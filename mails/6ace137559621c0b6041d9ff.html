YouTube Overtakes TV as Top Source of Political News (2 minute read)

YouTube is now the most-used individual source for political and election news in the US at 16.5%, narrowly ahead of TV news at 16.1%. Social platforms collectively lead traditional news sources 60% to 32%, while 61% of Americans trust independent news creators as much as professional journalists on political topics. Credibility depends more on reporting than credentials, with 67% saying the quality of the reporting matters more than formal journalism training. Publicly correcting mistakes is the strongest trust signal, with 86% saying it increases their trust in independent creators.
The state of AI in marketing: Q4 2026 (6 minute read)

More than 90% of marketers use AI, but many still rely on copying and pasting outputs instead of integrating AI into their workflows. Disconnected tools and poor data quality limit its effectiveness, while generating more content creates extra review work and can hurt customer experience. Teams need better workflow integration and data governance to realize AI's business value.

Strategies & Tactics

Meta creative analysis with Claude (1 minute read)

Analyze Meta ad creative performance by exporting daily ad data to a CSV and uploading it to Claude or ChatGPT. Generate 3 charts to track monthly creative churn, weekly production versus hit rate, and spend share from ads under 30 days old. Use 20-30% monthly churn and a 10-15% hit rate as benchmarks. If older ads still drive significant spend or hit rates fall while production stays flat, refresh creative before performance declines.
Don't teach customers everything before they buy (1 minute read)

Improve retention by separating pre-purchase education from post-purchase guidance. Before buying, explain features that build confidence in the decision. After buying, share tips that help customers get more value over time. Teaching customers things they didn't know about owning a product encourages long-term engagement.

How Brands Are Skipping Gen Z (5 minute read)

Brands can stay relevant without chasing Gen Z trends or trying to look young. Older consumers offer greater spending power and less competition for attention. Tactics include creating distinctive mascots and featuring diverse ages and skin types. Brands can also control their retail experience and design products for everyday use. Choosing relevant celebrities and highlighting scientific expertise can build credibility without relying on influencers.
