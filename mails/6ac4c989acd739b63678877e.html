Building is cheap. Owning it is not (10 minute read)

While modern tools and AI make building software products cheaper, the cost of ownership and making strategic choices remains high. Dedicated PMs add value by focusing on business viability, ranking roadmap bets, tracking clear predictions, and acting as communication bridges and quality gatekeepers for their teams.
The Future of Knowledge Inside the Enterprise (10 minute read)

Enterprise knowledge management creates business value by connecting organizational experience to AI agents acting under human accountability. Establishing a context layer over existing systems extends expertise, improves efficiency, enhances verification, and strengthens continuity through a structured operating model.

Users don't hate change. They hate you (6 minute read)

Users do not inherently hate change - they resent updates that destroy their familiarity and control without clear benefit. Citing psychological research on loss aversion, the article explains that users weigh losses in comfort far more than potential gains, criticizing tech companies for forcing redesigns without explaining their value.
The fake double diamond is dead. The real double diamond is immortal (6 minute read)

The product development dichotomy between upfront definition and continuous iteration is a false choice. True innovation requires balancing upfront discovery with ongoing observation within a cohesive system. Relying on artificial shortcuts or AI-driven alignment ultimately degrades software quality and prevents teams from solving complex problems.
