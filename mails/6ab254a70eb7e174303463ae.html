News & Trends

Caring vs. Knowing (5 minute read)

While AI lowers software development costs, supporting a growing audience demands continuous learning and quality standards. Developers face challenges in managing user diversity and the risks of unchecked customization. Ultimately, maintaining quality requires an ongoing commitment.
The stakeholder who matters most (6 minute read)

The industry overlooks the primary stakeholder: the user. Current evaluation metrics measure machine functionality rather than whether an agent actually reduces effort for humans. Consequently, companies fail to instrument the complete customer journey, leading to hidden inference costs and unmeasured user abandonment.

Opinions & Tutorials

‘Not enough usage': your biggest churn reason is a broken habit loop (16 minute read)

"Not enough usage" is the primary driver of subscription app churn, often accounting for 26% to 40% of cancellations. This churn reason is frequently due to a broken habit loop rather than a pricing problem. Using discounts to fix usage churn is ineffective because lowering the price does not restore user value or engagement.
Not every question deserves a study (8 minute read)

Every research question should be assigned a specific price in minutes, days, or an owned risk before any investigation begins. By implementing a five-step routing discipline, product teams can determine whether an answer already exists in organizational memory, warrants a rapid study, or should be bypassed entirely with an accepted risk written down.
