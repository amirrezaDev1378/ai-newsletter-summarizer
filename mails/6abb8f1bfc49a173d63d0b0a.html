Stop managing AI agents like software. Start managing them like new hires (3 minute read)

Organizations fail to deploy enterprise AI agents because they treat them like software rollouts rather than new hires needing onboarding and data prep. Clean data, workflow integration, and governance present far greater bottlenecks than model performance.
Google has the best AI money can buy. Its products still feel broken (3 minute read)

Google's search quality failures stem from product and organizational flaws rather than model limitations. By layering probabilistic AI features onto a deterministic search interface without updating UI promises or user feedback loops, companies risk eroding user trust through unverified AI outputs.
