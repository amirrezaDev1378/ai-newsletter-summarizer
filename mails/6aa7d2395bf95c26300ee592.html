Big Tech & Startups

Top AI Leaders Call for Slowing Down AI Development (5 minute read)

Anthropic's CEO recently released a 3,800-word essay calling for a global slowdown of AI development. He said that while the technology offers many benefits, it is advancing too quickly for researchers to continue safely. AI leaders are preparing for a day when an AI system can grow more advanced without the aid of human researchers. Some researchers believe that achieving recursive self-improvement will lead to AI systems spinning out of control.
OpenAI's Sam Altman says it would be ‘ill-advised' to go public in 2026 (1 minute read)

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has confirmed that the company will not be going public this year. He said it would be an ill-advised moment to go public due to current safety concerns. OpenAI had hired bankers and lawyers with the goal of going public this year. The company is now leaning toward 2027 due to the volatility of tech stocks and its own financial challenges.

Where Has Construction Automation Been Successful? (20 minute read)

Construction is labor-intensive. There has, unsurprisingly, long been interest in automating the process to reduce the amount of labor required. However, automation has only been successful in tasks that can be done off-site in factories and on-site tasks that are extremely factory-like. Almost nothing outside of these two categories has succeeded.
A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics (6 minute read)

The push by AI companies to solve mathematical problems as a benchmark is detrimental to the science of mathematics. The goals of AI companies and the mathematical community are severely misaligned. This is a symptom of broader alignment issues that impact other scientific and creative professions, as well as the whole of society.

We Must Pace the Frontier (23 minute read)

While AI has the potential to produce a long line of technological miracles, like many technologies before it, it brings risk. These risks include losing control of AI systems, the misuse of the technology for cyberattacks and bioterrorism, and serious economic disruption. A race to the bottom spurred by commercial incentives makes these risks more acute. Anthropic's CEO has called for the industry to prioritize caution over speed and prudence over profit. Addressing these risks will require pacing the rate of capabilities advancement so that risk prevention has time to keep up.
Roblox is making it easier to build games with AI — and play them outside Roblox (4 minute read)

The company behind Roblox announced several new features at its annual Roblox Developer Conference, including new game-creation tools, expanded NPC capabilities, and the ability to make games available across platforms. The company is also launching a dedicated card and wallet for creators. Roblox is rolling out its generative AI game creation feature more broadly to Serbia and Singapore. It is also gaining desktop access, and later this year, it will get a new prompt-based scene-generating feature.
