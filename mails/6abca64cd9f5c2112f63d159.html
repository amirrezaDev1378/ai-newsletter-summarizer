What if we use AI to ship more of the *right* things, instead of just *more* things? Most ideas online focus on shipping faster, increasing throughput, or reducing cycle time. I would like to see more stories of AI used to: • Get signal from product analytics to understand feature adoption, cohorts, and blockers, and prioritize work. • Make customer success chats immediately actionable, and draft solutions. • Do social and online "listening" to catch what users are saying about your product. • Autonomously intercept bugs and outages from telemetry, and draft fixes. Let's ship the right things!
