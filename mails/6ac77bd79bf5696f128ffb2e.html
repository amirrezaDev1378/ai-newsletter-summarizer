News & Trends

Young Entrepreneurs Are Delaying Domain Adoption, Not Abandoning It (3 minute read)

Young entrepreneurs are starting businesses on social platforms before investing in websites and domains once they gain traction. Among 18- to 29-year-olds, domain registration interest fell from 55% in spring 2023 to 17% in spring 2026. Websites remain valued for credibility and permanence, while search visibility is a major purchase driver. More than half say AI-assisted website builders would make adoption more appealing by reducing the cost and complexity of building a web presence.
The next hurdle for AI agents: getting websites to let them in (5 minute read)

AI agents like Muse and ChatGPT are able to complete tasks such as shopping and booking for users but are often blocked by websites' anti-bot systems. Some blocks are intentional while others mistakenly flag agents as malicious bots. Meta, Walmart, Stripe, and others are developing an open standard to distinguish authorized AI agents from bad bots and give businesses more control over agent access.

Strategies & Tactics

Hero-framed case studies (2 minute read)

Hero framing turns a case study into a human-led story by making a specific customer the central character. Start with their problem and transformation, then use measurable outcomes as proof. The body explains how the product helped them achieve those results. This makes the case study more relatable and gives audiences someone to celebrate.
How to plan 2027 when traffic is down and AI won't attribute (5 minute read)

Build the 2027 marketing plan around AI visibility, brand mentions, community participation, branded search, and other signals that happen before a website visit. Start by identifying which AI engines, communities, publications, and sources influence buyers in your category, then budget specific hours and owners for digital PR, off site content, community work, and proprietary research. Measure exposure, branded search, and business outcomes together each month because no single metric captures influence when attribution is incomplete. Fund proven tactics at full capacity, cap new experiments with clear owners and kill dates, and reallocate the budget each quarter based on what the three layer measurement shows.

Performance Max and Advantage+ Grade Their Own Homework (7 minute read)

Meta Advantage+ and Google Performance Max should be treated as optimization tools, not independent proof of advertising impact, because the platforms control buying, attribution, and reporting. Optmyzr found Performance Max overlapped with existing Search keywords in 91% of 503 Google Ads accounts. The same problem appears in Meta and Google reporting because platforms can count modeled conversions and prioritize brand searches, existing customers, and other users who were already likely to convert. When making budget decisions, rely on randomized incrementality tests, auditable holdouts, finance reconciliations, and visibility into what the system actually bought rather than platform-reported ROAS alone.

Miscellaneous

Brands Using Packaging To Disrupt An Industry (5 minute read)

Packaging is one of the few marketing channels that keeps working after the sale, since a product left on the counter or posted online gets seen again for free. Graza's squeeze bottles made olive oil easier to cook with and recognizable from across an aisle. One Trick Pony flipped its peanut butter jar upside down to fix the stirring problem, which also sets it apart on the shelf. Juliet's cylinders strip out the cues that make boxed wine look cheap. The common thread is packaging that changes how a product is used or perceived, not just how it looks.
Glastonbury vs The Internet: what r/glastonbury_festival did when tickets sold out (7 minute read)

Glastonbury's Reddit community shows how brands can turn high-stress moments into stronger loyalty by giving people a place to share experiences and help each other. After tickets sold out in 42 minutes, the subreddit created a dedicated thread for winners and naturally enforced norms that kept celebrations from alienating people who missed out. Roughly one in six comments focused on missing confirmation emails, yet experienced ticket buyers quickly answered questions and reassured anxious strangers. The result was a community that provided practical support, shared expertise, and even connected strangers who ended up buying tickets and attending the festival together.
Why Marketers Are Acting Like Nightclub Promoters (4 minute read)

Brands are competing for consumer and creator attention with rave-like events and extravagant trips designed to create experiences worth sharing. Events increasingly function as content-generation engines that reach audiences beyond attendees through social feeds. As brand events become more common, standard cocktail parties and brunches struggle to stand out.
