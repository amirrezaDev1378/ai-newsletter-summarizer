News & Trends

The harms of short-form video on the brain are starting to show (5 minute read)

Heavy short-form video use may impair attention and memory. A meta-analysis of about 70 studies found stronger links to cognitive problems than mental health issues, with compulsive use predicting worse outcomes. Other research links more phone time to poorer working memory and uncontrolled short-form use to weaker episodic memory. Memory and imagination share neural circuitry, so these effects could also make it harder to envision and plan for the future.
Has mainstream culture disappeared? New YouTube trends research says no (3 minute read)

Mainstream culture isn't disappearing, but brands can no longer rely on celebrities or broad media to create cultural relevance. YouTube creators and memes are now seen as widely known as Hollywood stars. Cultural momentum often starts in niche online communities before spreading through sharing and creating. YouTube says the platform can turn that momentum into broader cultural validation, with 65% of respondents saying something feels more real or legitimate when they see it there.

Strategies & Tactics

"Proof of humanity" is the biggest marketing opportunity right now (3 minute read)

66% of people under 30 already react negatively when a business visibly uses AI in its ads, a reversal for the generation assumed to be AI's biggest fans. That backlash is turning restraint itself into a content opportunity, since publicly declaring what a brand won't automate can work as marketing in its own right, not just a compliance footnote. Aerie's pledge to skip AI-generated models became its most-liked Instagram post ever, with a 23% sales bump the same quarter.
Are We Building Brands for a World That No Longer Exists? (5 minute read)

Brands still rely on growth tactics built for a simpler, more stable world. This piece argues for a “both/and” approach. Keep evidence-based marketing principles while accounting for trust, context, and long-term consequences. Set rules against manipulative tactics. Use brand touchpoints to explain real-world decisions. Dedicate 10% of activity to retention, employee pride, and referrals.

Does Retail Media Add Value? (2 minute read)

Retail media only adds value when ads drive profitable incremental sales. For advertisers, high ROAS can be misleading if shoppers would have purchased anyway. Retailers can also hurt margins if ads displace organic conversions or degrade the shopping experience. Both sides should measure whether advertising changes outcomes.
Are you doing marketing…or building software? (7 minute read)

95% of enterprise GenAI projects show zero return. The biggest gap is in areas with strong off-the-shelf options, such as rank tracking, citation monitoring, and content scoring. Vendors serving thousands of customers have already solved these integration challenges. Custom workflows are harder to outsource, but buying proven expertise can still beat learning through trial and error.
