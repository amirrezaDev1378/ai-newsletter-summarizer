We Checked 56 of America's Biggest Brands: Newsrooms Lock AI Out, Everyone Else Leaves the Door Open (10 minute read)

68% of major US brands have no AI-specific crawling rules. Newsrooms are more likely to block AI bots, with 75% restricting at least one compared with 6% of other brands. None of the 52 accessible robots.txt files fully blocked OpenAI's or Anthropic's AI search bots by name, though some blocked training crawlers. Forbes blocks training bots while allowing AI search, while GEICO explicitly allows all 12 AI crawlers it names.

Strategies & Tactics

How we increased orders by 9% without a discount code (2 minute read)

A South American gift company increased orders by 9% by adding 2 free services to its checkout summary without offering a discount. Research showed customers hesitated at the final price and wanted better value. The test highlighted existing benefits, including premium gift wrapping and a satisfaction guarantee, with both listed as free beside the total. This reframed the purchase around what customers received rather than just what they paid.
Churn Happens Early (6 minute read)

SaaS churn is heavily front-loaded, with the biggest drop-off often happening during signup and initial product use before retention stabilizes. Track retention by signup cohort rather than total user churn to identify where new users leave and prioritize fixes with the greatest downstream impact. In one example, reducing signup-to-first-project churn could generate 164 additional paywall views compared with 59 from fixing the paywall step itself. Improve early experiences by helping users reach value milestones quickly, since every user lost early reduces the pool available for later conversions and referrals.

Claude Citations: Claude Cites the Page That Looks Like the Question (5 minute read)

Claude's citations closely match query format. Best-X questions pulled listicles 90% of the time. A-vs-B questions pulled comparison pages 87% of the time, while how-to questions pulled guides 85% of the time. Create content for each buyer query type and publish competitor comparison pages to avoid losing visibility to competitors. Sonnet and Haiku often cited different URLs for the same questions, so check both models to assess Claude visibility.

Miscellaneous

Not Sure What To Launch Next? Start Talking to Your Customers (7 minute read)

Study how customers use existing products to uncover winning product ideas rather than chasing trends or competitor launches. Zouk created its bestselling office bag after noticing working women needed one bag for laptops and personal items. Observe customers in real-world settings and identify workarounds that reveal unmet needs. Turn recurring complaints into product briefs and call customers to understand their frustrations. Solve functional problems first, then layer in design and cultural relevance.
Before You Rebrand: How a Brand Audit Reveals What Actually Needs to Change (5 minute read)

Conduct a brand audit before rebranding to identify the real problem. Compare internal strategy with customer feedback to determine whether the issue requires clearer messaging or a new identity. Review public touchpoints to find gaps between the intended brand and customer perception. Match each finding to the right fix rather than defaulting to a full redesign. Preserve existing brand equity and ensure the customer experience supports the brand promise.
