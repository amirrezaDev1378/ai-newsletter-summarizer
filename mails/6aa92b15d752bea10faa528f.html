Why Machine Learning Research Agents Don't Overfit, and What Compression Has to Do with It (13 minute read)

Successful research strategies often survive an extreme information bottleneck, suggesting that they capture real structure instead of memorizing validation data. Across eight datasets, fresh agents reproduced most results from prompts as short as 32 tokens, while deliberately overfit gains disappeared after compression.
When LLM Judges Agree, Should We Believe Them? (7 minute read)

Agreement among LLM judges can overstate confidence when models share prompts, architectures, or blind spots. A dependence-aware aggregation method models both judge reliability and pairwise correlations, improving accuracy by 9% to 14% over weighted majority voting across three evaluation tasks.
Agentic Coding Is Straining CI. Here's How We Scaled Test Impact Analysis at Anthropic (5 minute read)

Agentic coding drove a 25x increase in CI jobs over six months, overwhelming a singleton test selection service despite bigger machines, package sharding, and daily restarts. Anthropic replaced it with stateless listener workers, a journal in an in-memory store, and a separate rollup consumer, creating a horizontally scalable system that has remained stable.
