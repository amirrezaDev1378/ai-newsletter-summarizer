OpenAI Employee Arrested Over Hidden Recording Device (2 minute read)

OpenAI said it alerted the FBI after finding a hidden recording device in an executive conference room, leading to the firing and arrest of employee Abdulrahman Alfozan. Prosecutors alleged the device was used to covertly intercept conversations, while OpenAI said it found no evidence that model weights, source code, or customer data were accessed.
Gemini 4 Argon Lands on Google Cloud (3 minute read)

Google made Gemini 4 Argon available on Google Cloud as a frontier model for long, multi-step enterprise work. It was positioned for tasks requiring sustained context and domain expertise, including software development, finance, legal work, and cybersecurity.

Arguments About AI Consciousness Remain Highly Motivated and At Best Overconfident (14 minute read)

The debate over AI consciousness continues with minimal progress, as many argue without strong evidence. Anthropic's new policy classifies abusive behavior towards AI, such as Claude, as a violation of terms to cultivate positive model interactions. Despite skepticism from many, some, including Nick Bostrom, believe current AIs may be conscious, complicating ethical considerations around AI development.
How Anthropic's Dynamic Workflows Coordinate Claude Agents (6 minute read)

Splitting a task among several AI agents creates another job: deciding who does what, passing information between them, and checking their results. Anthropic's dynamic workflows let Claude write the program that manages those steps. An October 12 demonstration assigned stock tickers to separate agents, then reviewed their findings before producing a report. The presenters said this found more detail but cost much more than using one agent. Developers can set a session spending limit, but the running model does not know that budget.

NVIDIA Run:ai Is Now Open Source as Part of NVIDIA DSX AI Factory Platform (17 minute read)

Nvidia is making the full NVIDIA Run:ai platform available as open-source software. It is now possible for every NVIDIA DSX ecosystem partner and AI cloud to increase workload throughput, improve GPU utilization, and operate shared AI infrastructure more efficiently and effectively. Frontier Models for Every Robot (6 minute read)

Physical Intelligence is making its latest models available for training and inference through the Pi API beta. The Pi API adapts the latest and most powerful robotic foundation models to each application, dealing with ML infrastructure, compute, and complex data pipelines. Users just upload data, launch fine-tuning on managed compute and infrastructure, and access the fine-tuned models via a hosted gateway to run inference.
LFM2.5-24B-A2B (7 minute read)

LFM2.5-24B-A2B, a 24B-parameter reasoning model with 2B active parameters, is designed for fast, private on-device agents. It outperforms or matches larger models like Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on five out of eight benchmarks and runs up to 1.7x faster due to its efficient architecture. Available on Hugging Face, it supports extensive inference frameworks, making it suitable for tasks needing swift local inference and tool usage.

Programmatic clients will autodiscover the local service and just work. OpenCode can now be used as a library with the SDK package. More details about the update are available in the article.
