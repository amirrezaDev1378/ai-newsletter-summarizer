Should you start with a PRD or a prototype? (11 minute read)

This article examines whether product teams should start with a PRD or a prototype, arguing they should evolve together in a learning loop focused on the core unknown. Although AI tools can quickly generate both, teams must maintain intentionality, aggressively curate features, and provide careful human oversight to match actual product intent.
Human in the loop means making sure the human is not out of the loop (10 minute read)

Human-in-the-loop systems often fail when oversight is reduced to automated confirm clicks or post-hoc approvals, turning human reviewers into moral crumple zones. Effective agency design requires clear mandates, permissions-based constraints, and keeping users involved at points where they can genuinely stop runs or reverse irreversible actions.
