AI Product Manager Salary in 2026 (5 minute read)

Compensation varies widely based on employer and seniority, with top employers like OpenAI paying median packages up to $750,000. Total compensation is heavily influenced by stock equity. A candidate's job level dictates their salary band rather than negotiation.
Predictability vs outcomes (4 minute read)

Product development fails to achieve meaningful outcomes because companies prioritize corporate predictability over innovation. Prioritizing predictability leads teams to adopt rigid project models that sacrifice discovery. To achieve better results, organizations must acknowledge uncertainty, embrace humility in product discovery, and reward outcomes over fixed plans.
