News & Trends

TikTok's Agent Checkout Enters the Race (3 minute read)

TikTok announced Shopping Assistant and Buy Direct at Advertising Week NYC on October 5, both now in eligibility-based testing in the US. Shopping Assistant is a conversational agent that handles product details, sizing, and availability, then hands the purchase to the in-app browser. Buy Direct is a one-click checkout inside the For You feed using stored payment and address details. Both run on Google's Universal Commerce Protocol, and the brand remains the merchant of record.
The evidence is clear: AI search is costing publishers (4 minute read)

AI search is reducing the traffic publishers receive from traditional search while AI referrals remain too small to make up the difference. One study found AI Overviews reduced outbound organic clicks by 39.8%. Pew found traditional-result clicks fell from 15% to 8% when an AI summary appeared. Meanwhile, ChatGPT generated just 0.02% of publisher referrals compared with 7.3% from Google Search.

Strategies & Tactics

4 SEO strategies for AI-driven brand search (1 minute read)

Google now shows AI Overviews for roughly 80% of branded searches. Keep brand messaging consistent across owned channels so AI systems get clear context, then create content to fill gaps where third-party sources currently shape answers. Monitor competitor content and review sites because negative claims can influence AI-generated brand answers. An accurate AI instructions page can also provide a direct source for brand information and become a frequently cited page in AI Overviews and AI Mode.
When to do sales remotely vs in-person (2 minute read)

The best mix of remote and in-person sales meetings depends on product complexity. Using the right mix can improve sales performance by up to 27.3%. For simple products, about 37.9% of meetings held in person produced the best results, while complex products performed worse when sales teams relied only on remote meetings. In-person meetings help build trust and uncover customer needs, which matters more when products require more customization or evaluation. Sales teams should use more in-person meetings early in complex sales relationships, then reduce them as they learn the customer's needs, while still accommodating customers who prefer remote meetings.
5 tips for optimizing existing content for ChatGPT (2 minute read)

Existing content can be optimized for ChatGPT without publishing new posts. Lead with the answer before adding context so key claims are easier to extract. Use clear statements instead of hedging. Mention relevant brands and competitors to provide useful context. Turn headings into specific questions that mirror user queries. Refresh old posts with new data, screenshots, and sections rather than simply changing the publication date.

How to write a great experiment plan (12 minute read)

An experiment plan should prevent costly errors, not serve as a ceremony. Start with an evidence-based hypothesis and one primary metric, then size the test around the smallest change worth shipping. Count only users who reached the change, since including everyone assigned dilutes the effect. Before launch, agree on stopping thresholds and on what happens under each outcome, so that early peeks don't drive the decision. After launch, confirm the traffic split holds.

Miscellaneous

Adam Harris on Why Follower Count Is the New Nielsen Box (5 minute read)

Follower count is becoming a poor measure of creator value as platforms increasingly serve content based on interests rather than follows. Adam Harris, co-founder of creator platform Gaggl, says 70% of video views now come from non-followers. Gaggl's campaign data also shows micro-creators generate 5X more engagement than larger creators. Larger creators remain useful for awareness while smaller creators drive deeper engagement. Harris recommends starting with small creator tests before scaling based on results.
LLMs are quietly watermarking AI content. Now what? (6 minute read)

AI labs are watermarking generated content by subtly changing word choices during generation. These tiny changes accumulate across longer outputs and can survive copy-paste and light edits. The bigger concern is AEO because labs may filter AI-generated content from training data to avoid model degradation. Teams should monitor branded and non-branded AI prompts to separate retrieved from trained-in visibility. Audit how much content is fully AI-generated and prioritize human rewriting for content that needs to influence future model knowledge.
How to survive a double life as an operator-creator (9 minute read)

Operator-creators run a flywheel where the day job makes the content credible and the audience becomes a distribution channel for the employer. Elena Verna keeps it working by disclosing everything to her employer, audience, and sponsors, ideally before the first day of work. She also sets boundaries up front, keeping the audience as her own while giving Lovable work first claim on her time.
