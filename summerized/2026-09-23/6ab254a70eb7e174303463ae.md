### [The stakeholder who matters most (6 minute read)](https://convivastage.wpengine.com/resource/i-gave-two-talks-at-dreamforce-a-show-where-the-stakeholder-who-matters-most-never-came-up/)

## Opinions & Tutorials

### [‘Not enough usage': your biggest churn reason is a broken habit loop (16 minute read)](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/not-enough-usage-churn)
"Not enough usage" is the primary driver of subscription app churn, often accounting for 26% to 40% of cancellations. This churn reason is frequently due to a broken habit loop rather than a pricing problem. Using discounts to fix usage churn is ineffective because lowering the price does not restore user value or engagement.

### [Not every question deserves a study (8 minute read)](https://www.thevoiceofuser.com/not-every-question-deserves-a-study/)
Every research question should be assigned a specific price in minutes, days, or an owned risk before any investigation begins. By implementing a five-step routing discipline, product teams can determine whether an answer already exists in organizational memory, warrants a rapid study, or should be bypassed entirely with an accepted risk written down.

### [The Problem With Putting People in Boxes (6 minute read)](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-440-the-problem-with-putting)

### [AI on Top of a Dysfunctional System (1): The Product Backlog (14 minute read)](https://www.scrum.org/resources/blog/ai-top-dysfunctional-system-1-product-backlog)
Applying generative AI to a dysfunctional product backlog masks underlying flaws by polishing artifacts without resolving root causes like missing customer evidence or decision authority. AI-generated user stories obscure a lack of real product discovery. To remain effective, product teams must maintain clear direction, authentic authority, and feedback loops tied to real outcomes.
