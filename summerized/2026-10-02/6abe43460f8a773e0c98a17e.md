### [Why Instinct Feels Magical (9 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FKAqwsT/1/010001a0f736bb0e-6dab6fac-733c-4cc1-921a-5388c47c90b8-000000/_Qdhpp6aY9QrvcqAXycH8a_41Q3KMMq9eXFpOEp8Mr8=452)

### [How We Rebuilt Our Design Language in Three Days with AI (13 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fblog.curiosity.ai%2Fblog%2F2026-09-design-language-in-three-days%3Futm_source=tldrdev/1/010001a0f736bb0e-6dab6fac-733c-4cc1-921a-5388c47c90b8-000000/DaLXi5M9y2rG-93NfIbHyxDr65GskiVB01wDljVxX0s=452)
A small team used tightly specified briefs and coding agents to rebuild a 67-page site across 22 visual directions, then turned the strongest ideas into one design system. The process worked because content stayed fixed, rules were numerical, real pages were reviewed, automated checks were themselves verified, and losing experiments were deleted.
