## Science & Futuristic Technology

### [Google figures out how to watermark AI-designed proteins (11 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/09/google-figures-out-how-to-watermark-ai-designed-proteins/)

Google's DeepMind team has developed a method for watermarking protein sequences without compromising their biological function. The watermark is permanent, resilient against exporting, resizing, and other modifications, and could help DNA synthesizers verify the safety and origin of their sources.

### [Most powerful obesity drug yet: People lost up to 25% of weight in trial (5 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/health/2026/09/most-powerful-obesity-drug-yet-people-lost-up-to-25-of-weight-in-trial/)

Late-stage clinical trial results for the obesity drug retatrutide showed an average body weight loss of 25% after 90 weeks, with over a third of participants losing 30% or more. The drug also reduced knee pain and sleep apnea events, improved cardiometabolic markers, and resolved prediabetes in over 90% of the participants who had the condition at the start.
