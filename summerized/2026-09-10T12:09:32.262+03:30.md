### 🛠️ Engineering and Applications

**[Benchmarking LiDAR SLAM on Livox Mid-360 Datasets](https://github.com/harshareddy1611/livox-mid360-slam-evaluation?utm_source=tldrhardware)**
This open-source project evaluates four popular SLAM algorithms (KISS-ICP, DLIO, FAST-LIO2, and GLIM) using a Jetson Orin NX and aerial drone data. The benchmark compares trajectory accuracy against millimeter-grade VICON ground-truth, helping engineers choose the best state estimation tools for compute-constrained platforms.

**[Xiaomi 18 Fold beats Apple's iPhone Fold to market](https://www.stuff.tv/hot-stuff/xiaomi-has-beaten-apple-to-the-punch-and-might-have-the-iphone-fold-bested-for-hardware-too/?utm_source=tldrhardware)**

### 🧠 Research and Prototypes

**[RoboTok: Internet-Scale Data Engine for Robot Learning](https://rice-robotpi-lab.github.io/RoboTok/?utm_source=tldrhardware)**
Rice University researchers created RoboTok, an automated engine that processes human demonstration videos from the internet to train robot manipulation. By extracting trajectories and interactions from uncurated web content, the system overcomes the expensive bottleneck of physical data collection for AI.

**[Orion: A DIY 12-DOF Quadruped Robot Dog](https://github.com/AshishA26/Orion-Quadruped?utm_source=tldrhardware)**
Orion is an open-source, 3D-printed robot dog built using ROS 2 and serial bus servos. The repository includes CAD models, kinematic calculations, and firmware guides, providing an accessible architecture for teams prototyping low-cost legged locomotion.
