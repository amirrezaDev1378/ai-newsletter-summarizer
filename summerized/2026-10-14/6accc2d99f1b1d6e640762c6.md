## Strategies & Tactics

* **[How we increased orders by 9% without a discount code (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fconversion-rate-experts.com%2Ftwo-free-services-win-report%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a12fd915e9-be87d52f-cd28-4e66-b5c2-c81615c1e244-000000/eUjQgswg-p_uMyWk89JGYxoWpx4aYKAbTZJgP6g6BkQ=452)**
  A South American gift company grew orders by 9% simply by adding two existing free services (premium gift wrapping and a satisfaction guarantee) directly into the checkout summary next to the total. This helped alleviate final-price hesitation and successfully reframed the purchase around value received rather than cost.

* **[Churn Happens Early (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.valuepaths.com%2Factivation%2Fchurn-happens-early%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a12fd915e9-be87d52f-cd28-4e66-b5c2-c81615c1e244-000000/fKlwM2o9G0_r1XGVucsvRdHSyno6hEmJxpIm_u4EqFg=452)**
  SaaS churn is front-loaded, with the steepest drop-offs occurring during signup and initial product interactions. Marketers and product teams should track retention by signup cohort to find leak points. Helping users reach early value milestones prevents downstream user loss, which ultimately yields a larger pool of potential long-term customers.
