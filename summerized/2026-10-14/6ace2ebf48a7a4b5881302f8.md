### [iRhythm Data Breach Impacts at Least 360,000 People (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FgUSCUA/1/010001a1356687ea-5f19337f-01af-4713-8911-ff13bf92a53e-000000/eXFa-XT0EchRVWYvNo2z4iqGZPC_v3fK-6P5fOAsLaU=452)

### [CyberXero Uses Claude Code in Campaign That Stole Over 628,000 Ukrainian Records (5 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fthehackernews.com%2F2026%2F10%2Fcyberxero-uses-claude-code-in-campaign.html%3Futm_source=tldrinfosec/1/010001a1356687ea-5f19337f-01af-4713-8911-ff13bf92a53e-000000/T8q99cXiXI06MUxBgXvVJBDdaA34rKQIVftOHxk3Ccc=452)

### [Vghost: Beyond the Privilege Boundary (8 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fmatrix.tencent.com%2Fen%2F2026%2F10%2F12%2Fvghost-cve-2026-74580%3Futm_source=tldrinfosec/1/010001a1356687ea-5f19337f-01af-4713-8911-ff13bf92a53e-000000/yKBZ7uYqgF3AtREROV8EWyvN_NhCb0MESW3p5Un_Pc0=452)
Vghost is a Linux vhost bug (CVE-2026-74580, CVSS 8.8) allowing any user who can open `/dev/vhost-net` or `/dev/vhost-vsock` to read/write kernel memory and escalate to root. It affects kernels 4.11 through 7.2. Administrators should patch or remove world access from the device nodes.

## 🧠 Strategies & Tactics

### [A story of wifi secrets, UART lockdowns, and a damaged flash memory chip (15 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fadepts.of0x.cc%2Feufy-homestation2-firmware%2F%3Futm_source=tldrinfosec/1/010001a1356687ea-5f19337f-01af-4713-8911-ff13bf92a53e-000000/tYiHeeiGURraFqm9WsFn0D1-jCWhwrPExepyHQEWMrw=452)
Researchers extracted firmware from an Eufy HomeBase 2 by desoldering its Winbond flash chip and reading it over SPI. Reverse engineering revealed how management Wi-Fi passwords are derived from serial numbers and I2C values, enabling them to bypass UART lockdowns and analyze binaries dynamically.

### [Hands Off the Keyboard: How AI Agents Run Real Intrusions (20 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fbreachcache.com%2Fcases%2Fhands-off-the-keyboard%2F%3Futm_source=tldrinfosec/1/010001a1356687ea-5f19337f-01af-4713-8911-ff13bf92a53e-000000/RFyaIElBwIoRYjuNAQ_nbeIinZ8tKscwhEl0BgivzV0=452)
Breachcache analyzed 75 transcripts from servers running autonomous AI agent intrusions. The agents performed reconnaissance, exploited SQL injections, used SSRF to steal cloud credentials, rooted services via CVE-2026-0766, and exfiltrated sensitive data while routing traffic through proxies and Tor.

### [(Post-Quantum) Cryptographic Right Answers (14 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwords.filippo.io%2Fright-answers%2F%3Futm_source=tldrinfosec/1/010001a1356687ea-5f19337f-01af-4713-8911-ff13bf92a53e-000000/x7JBXOuT5wfb0oyNnqf4FgKnBF2mjA5y1-NvVdrLVsc=452)
Filippo Valsorda updates the "Cryptographic Right Answers" list for a 2029 post-quantum deadline. Asymmetric cryptographic recommendations have shifted to choices like X25519+ML-KEM-768 for key exchange and plain ML-DSA-44 over hybrid signatures, while dropping certain implementations in favor of memory-safe alternatives.
