### [YouTube Overtakes TV as Top Source of Political News (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fnews.syr.edu%2F2026%2F10%2F08%2Fyoutube-overtakes-tv-as-top-source-of-political-news-study-finds%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a134fbf5be-5995611a-1a8c-4151-88ee-5cc4d0d693f6-000000/D7TKjSU2E9tJyPYY78T0Ir7xnlFzZktK0sTymdaud5M=452)

YouTube has surpassed television as the most-used individual source for political and election news in the US, capturing 16.5% compared to TV news's 16.1%. Social platforms collectively beat traditional news sources 60% to 32%. Meanwhile, 61% of Americans trust independent news creators as much as professional journalists on political topics. Credibility relies more on reporting than credentials, with 67% of respondents stating that the quality of reporting matters more than formal journalism training. Publicly correcting mistakes serves as the strongest trust signal, with 86% noting it increases their trust in independent creators.

Realizing AI's full business value requires improved workflow integration and proper data governance.

## 🚀 Strategies & Tactics

### [Meta creative analysis with Claude (1 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Fposts%2Fcurtishowland_easy-ai-way-to-run-meta-creative-analysis-share-7512647729584726017-BSCU%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a134fbf5be-5995611a-1a8c-4151-88ee-5cc4d0d693f6-000000/8__YtA2ULQ8X-upQqRAZtVG5YAUEcISC2r4FUNqt94o=452)

Marketers can evaluate Meta ad performance by exporting daily ad data to a CSV and uploading it to Claude or ChatGPT. By generating three specific charts—monthly creative churn, weekly production versus hit rate, and spend share from ads under 30 days old—teams can track their creative health. Use benchmarks of 20-30% monthly churn and a 10-15% hit rate. If older ads continue to command significant spend or hit rates drop while output stays flat, it's time to refresh creatives before performance declines.

### [Don't teach customers everything before they buy (1 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Fposts%2Fyojimmykim_a-strange-retention-mistake-teaching-customers-share-7515506493195042816-CiEs%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a134fbf5be-5995611a-1a8c-4151-88ee-5cc4d0d693f6-000000/3G-Sf5K7Ukfu36bxH9Wbw6jLVWLnc3LdUTzSHgrYvdM=452)

Customer retention can be improved by separating pre-purchase education from post-purchase guidance. Prior to purchase, focus on features that build buying confidence. After purchase, provide tips that help customers unlock ongoing value. Teaching customers unexpected details about product ownership after they buy fosters long-term engagement.
