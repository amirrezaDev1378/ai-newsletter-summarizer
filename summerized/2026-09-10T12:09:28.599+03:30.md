### 🛠️ Engineering and Applications

#### [Benchmarking LiDAR SLAM on Livox Mid-360 Datasets](https://github.com/harshareddy1611/livox-mid360-slam-evaluation)
This open-source project evaluates four popular SLAM algorithms (KISS-ICP, DLIO, FAST-LIO2, and GLIM) using aerial drone data collected with a Livox Mid-360 LiDAR. Tested on a Jetson Orin NX, the project compares trajectory accuracy against VICON ground-truth data to help engineers choose algorithms for compute-constrained platforms.

#### [Xiaomi 18 Fold beats Apple's iPhone Fold to market](https://www.stuff.tv/hot-stuff/xiaomi-has-beaten-apple-to-the-punch-and-might-have-the-iphone-fold-bested-for-hardware-too/)

### 🧠 Research and Prototypes

#### [RoboTok: Internet-Scale Data Engine for Dexterous Manipulation](https://rice-robotpi-lab.github.io/RoboTok/)
Researchers at Rice University developed RoboTok, an automated engine that processes uncurated human demonstration videos from the internet to train physical AI. By extracting hand trajectories and interactions from web video, the system overcomes the expensive bottleneck of physical data collection for robot training.

#### [Orion: A DIY 12-DOF Quadruped Robot Dog](https://github.com/AshishA26/Orion-Quadruped)
Orion is an open-source, 12-degree-of-freedom quadruped designed from scratch using ROS 2, 3D-printed parts, and serial bus servos. The repository includes full CAD models and kinematic calculations, serving as an accessible reference for low-cost legged locomotion platforms.
