* **[Amazon builds its 1,000th Leo satellite at 27 a week, but only 396 are in orbit (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farstechnica.com%2Fspace%2F2026%2F10%2Famazon-builds-1000th-satellite-is-weeks-away-from-space-internet-rollout%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a120d41bcd-6377f569-5fe8-431a-b309-698fafeceadb-000000/gujX5IaoPRRAmL0DoHZVqQTsAipuHl_1XWlFddPFABg=452)**

It builds propulsion in-house and borrowed consumer electronics methods to cut tests that took weeks down to hours. The bottleneck is launch: only 396 are in orbit against 3,232 needed, and the Vulcan grounding and a New Glenn pad explosion cost Amazon FCC spectrum priority.

## Engineering and Applications

* **[Anderon aims to industrialize quantum chip fabrication (5 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fspectrum.ieee.org%2Fanderon-quantum-fab%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a120d41bcd-6377f569-5fe8-431a-b309-698fafeceadb-000000/EzHxT5iFBFO7CnhWyLfQy2K48IHJzLr0B1g4kXU8Sy8=452)**
  IEEE Spectrum examines the up-to-$1B CHIPS R&D award finalized for IBM spinoff Anderon in September. IBM is matching that funding with $1B, while Anderon uses a 300mm Albany line and plans foundry services and process design kits for outside developers. The challenge is repeatable fabrication, including tighter control of Josephson junctions, rather than simply adding wafer capacity.

* **[Google open-sources ML Drift, a GPU engine that runs one shader set across OpenCL, Metal, WebGPU, and OpenGL ES (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fdevelopers.googleblog.com%2Fml-drift-next-gen-gpu-aiml-inference-at-the-edge%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a120d41bcd-6377f569-5fe8-431a-b309-698fafeceadb-000000/widTyw2vKLcdfINBXcXR6cSYgvxrco-5JdVWyTI3NCo=452)**
  Tensor virtualization decouples a tensor's logical layout from its physical GPU allocation, so one shader template replaces separate backend-specific codebases. For edge LLMs, ML Drift switches kernels between compute-bound prefill and memory-bound decode, using a convolution-aligned KV cache layout and in-kernel activation quantization to skip memory roundtrips. It cut YouTube Shorts frame latency by up to 40%, and Google co-optimized it with Arm, Intel, and Qualcomm GPUs.

## Research and Prototypes

* **[Supercapacitor cement is key to buildings that store their own power (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fnewatlas.com%2Fenergy%2Fcement-based-supercapacitor%2F%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a120d41bcd-6377f569-5fe8-431a-b309-698fafeceadb-000000/I5DRuQf2FoKAvSeW5KGldx7zLGlr8zwJBgLKXbDv5c4=452)**
  Scientists from the Harbin Institute of Technology developed a cement-based supercapacitor capable of powering small devices like LEDs. The device uses a 3D-printed electrode ink made from carbon nanotubes and cement, enabling fast energy discharge. Future research will enhance cold-weather performance, potentially allowing buildings to store energy and support smart environments.

* **[AWS Launches an Open-Source Physical AI Toolchain for Robotics (5 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.therobotreport.com%2Faws-launches-open-source-physical-ai-toolchain-for-robotics%2F%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a120d41bcd-6377f569-5fe8-431a-b309-698fafeceadb-000000/tKE0X_VLv4HfvfUQSu6gIgBBS41IhOnhnL54MRD64UE=452)**
  The toolchain covers synthetic data generation, training, simulation and validation, edge deployment and continuous improvement. It uses SageMaker for training and IoT Greengrass for pushing models to devices, plus NVIDIA's Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, GR00T, and Cosmos. AWS says it is not a direct replacement for RoboMaker, which shut down in 2025.
