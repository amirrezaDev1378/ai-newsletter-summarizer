* **[Building better public signals for AI impacts (7 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ffreesystems.substack.com%2Fp%2Fbuilding-better-public-signals-for%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a120663e74-ff47eb1e-6386-4c60-9982-257b1e385d8c-000000/eetiGhzGVSEHd-KRT3cPLqVoIKiFg2iH-IWxRoA4kYQ=452)**
  Analysis of 137,000 r/ChatGPT posts shows how users' experiences and expectations changed as ChatGPT became part of everyday life. Posts about therapy and companionship increased after the release of GPT-4o in May 2024 and reached about 10% of subreddit posts, while discussion of prompting declined as users became more familiar with AI. The researchers' real-time monitoring system could have detected the rise in emotional engagement by October 2024, months before broader discussion of sycophancy and AI psychosis. The findings suggest that Reddit can reveal emerging patterns in AI use and potential societal impacts that may not appear in traditional usage data.

* **[Big Tech is cutewashing its AI agents (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.fastcompany.com%2F91620091%2Fbig-tech-is-cutewashing-its-ai-agents%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a120663e74-ff47eb1e-6386-4c60-9982-257b1e385d8c-000000/6yw2rrFk8o2BZwHhys6OiU4j__U2QUEnsawP98yA53I=452)**
  Cuteness triggers the brain's pleasure centers before conscious thought, which makes people want to keep cute things close and more willing to forgive their mistakes. That makes big-eyed mascots a useful wrapper for AI agents that still hallucinate and need mass adoption. The same move made the early internet feel less intimidating in the late 1990s, then faded once the technology became routine. However, a look shared by every major AI company stops identifying any one of them and can become shorthand for what people distrust about the industry.

## Strategies & Tactics

* **[The Reason to Be on Reddit isn't for AEO (7 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fvermilioncliffs.substack.com%2Fp%2Fthe-reason-to-be-on-reddit-isnt-for%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a120663e74-ff47eb1e-6386-4c60-9982-257b1e385d8c-000000/yOVOjgvqv_IOcCRhQM2gTGB4QZj6KhXbJC0c34ShyS4=452)**
  Reddit is more valuable as a mid-funnel research channel than an AEO play because developers use it to compare tools and real-world experiences before buying. Treat it like earned media by joining existing conversations without pitching. Build credibility for about 3 months before approaching moderators about AMAs or sponsored events. Focus posts on problems and lessons rather than products. Brand accounts can work when they consistently teach. Measure impact through customer interviews since Reddit influence rarely appears in analytics.

* **[6 steps to building fast landing pages (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Fposts%2Fashleylewin_somewhere-right-now-paid-traffic-is-landing-share-7513616385541910528-TtYe%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a120663e74-ff47eb1e-6386-4c60-9982-257b1e385d8c-000000/PQnoKxBLLx58PKUJQ4TIL5YVK6yc89mu5OEDzMUASi4=452)**
  Instead of waiting weeks for overloaded web teams to build campaign pages, build lean landing pages in Claude Code and get them into market quickly. Start with a clear conversion problem, then write a 7-line brief and give AI a reusable context file covering brand voice, product details, customer language, and design rules. Ship at 70-80% polish while validating high-risk copy. Have stakeholders review a live preview, then hand off the approved page as a launch-ready bundle. If it performs, the web team rebuilds it properly.

* **[5 Things Content Marketers & SEOs Should Stop Doing in 2026 (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.kaleighmoore.com%2Fblog%2F2026%2F10%2F7%2Fcontent-marketing-for-aeo%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a120663e74-ff47eb1e-6386-4c60-9982-257b1e385d8c-000000/ForDCaSSJharkl0l653WFZdjPYxDDLsZqWqj17heqMs=452)**
  Zero-click search has made five common content habits counterproductive. Building pages around keywords misses how people now query, since the average ChatGPT prompt runs 23 words. Grading content on traffic ignores that the top-ranked page loses 58% of its CTR under an AI Overview. Publishing more matters less than maintaining what exists, as 75% of AI-cited pages were updated in the past year. The last two are relying on owned channels, when 85% of AI brand mentions come from third parties, and publishing generic guides with no original data.

* **[Google Search Data on Crawling, Indexing & Serving Timelines (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.seroundtable.com%2Fgoogle-crawling-indexing-serving-data-42225.html%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a120663e74-ff47eb1e-6386-4c60-9982-257b1e385d8c-000000/smJgGh--4UrzbgL2fhVVSzZ_kEiY_vF9pLxahogxSKk=452)**
  Google shared new internal data on how long crawling, indexing, and serving processes typically take. New URLs take about 20 hours to be discovered while known URLs take about 30 days to refresh. End-to-end indexing takes about 1.5 hours when processes run normally but can stretch to months or never. Title and snippet updates typically take 1-2 days, while site moves take 1-3 months. Core update recovery can take 3-6 months. Delays can stack because each process depends on the ones before it.

## Miscellaneous

* **[How We Catch Messaging Campaign Drift Before It Spreads (8 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fshiftmag.dev%2Fhow-we-catch-messaging-campaign-drift-before-it-spreads-11136%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a120663e74-ff47eb1e-6386-4c60-9982-257b1e385d8c-000000/NSCf0bK-R8j9o0ycZ-IznIYx8klH5TPVjn6KqV5Deko=452)**
  Campaign drift happens when the messages a business sends shift away from what was approved. This system for SMS and RCS converts each message into an embedding and matches it against clusters of previously judged traffic. Unmatched messages are grouped into new clusters, and an LLM issues one verdict per cluster against the approved campaign details rather than per message. Human reviewers confirm or override each flag before a sender is notified, restricted, or blocked.

* **[How Wrong Is Your Marketing Mix Model (MMM)? (21 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ftowardsdatascience.com%2Fhow-wrong-is-your-marketing-mix-model-mmm%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a120663e74-ff47eb1e-6386-4c60-9982-257b1e385d8c-000000/gNCULulov0z1Mmay9gVk3CqewTXHJbVK0BJ6F7M7Xwo=452)**
  MMM becomes more reliable when media budgets are deliberately phased, so channels move independently and spend reaches different levels. Build variation into the media plan by using tactics like weekly budget changes, dark periods, and peak spend periods instead of relying on model changes to solve weak data. In the simulation, the combined approach cut variance by 70% and bias by 47% while keeping each channel's annual budget unchanged. The tradeoff was a 3.42% loss in channel-driven revenue, so use lighter phasing when that cost is too high.
