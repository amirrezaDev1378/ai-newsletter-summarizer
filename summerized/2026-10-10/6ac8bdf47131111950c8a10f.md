## Opinions & Tutorials

### [Why Agent-Written Code Needs Product Verification (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fstack72.dev%2Fwhy-agent-written-code-needs-product-verification%2F%3Futm_source=tldrproduct/1/010001a12025f3b0-4642cf49-82a4-4905-8edd-a1308a82ebf2-000000/xxNc05IaGALl_tOf9zlYbOpl4-e_pfkw6GxLIuAVzCQ=452)
When AI agents write both code and tests, they encode identical misunderstandings, producing green builds that fail real user needs. Teams must establish independent verification based on user requirements. Lower maintenance costs and faster repair cycles make comprehensive end-to-end product verification feasible.

### [Tesler's Law: complexity moved to a different part of the journey (10 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2Fj1OCPG/1/010001a12025f3b0-4642cf49-82a4-4905-8edd-a1308a82ebf2-000000/P0PB9jv-e7AYQQJsP2ozvq-Plv1n1ElNctMSYUFipyU=452)
Generative AI interfaces do not eliminate software complexity - they shift it to users, developers, and reviewers. While AI automates routine tasks, it adds hidden burdens in prompt specification and verification. Designers must address this by creating clear constraints and verification workflows.

## Resources & Tools

### [Product definition (8 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fblog.ravi-mehta.com%2Fp%2Fproduct-definition%3Futm_source=tldrproduct/1/010001a12025f3b0-4642cf49-82a4-4905-8edd-a1308a82ebf2-000000/0tFU8ONcGBLm3-CvMqOmRuOKvEcvF_A--jUMmU4AhIQ=452)
Product thinking and "Product Definition" are essential in the AI era, where agents execute code rapidly given clear direction. Formerly known as feature specification, Product Definition shapes requirements and context into actionable artifacts like prototypes and PRDs. This competency evolves from tactical execution to building systems that align teams and AI agents.

### [When Does Onboarding Become a Product Problem? (8 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Famycmitchell.substack.com%2Fp%2Fproduct-management-developer-experience%3Futm_source=tldrproduct/1/010001a12025f3b0-4642cf49-82a4-4905-8edd-a1308a82ebf2-000000/AbdHL_wccUZhvKRqOkDd5jmBaZiazuVRVwjrIAZJAsE=452)
Customer onboarding friction often signals a hidden product issue. Product managers should address this by creating a repeatable "Golden Path," mapping user tasks from purchase to production to replace manual steps with automated features. Tracking time to first value and completion rates reduces delays and builds long-term product value.
