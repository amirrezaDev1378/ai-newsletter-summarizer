### [SpaceX Makes Big Play to Become a Wireless Carrier (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FISpXSa/1/010001a120525ad8-f8a5036d-d055-47fa-9623-977563e5eb28-000000/NnzSX-4sW-blcJQZE8N8kC7KUf0GLzGjbRIFQYRrmKM=452)

The 800-megahertz band licenses are tailored for wireless service from cellphone towers rather than satellites. SpaceX says the new spectrum will help cover places satellite links can't reach, though it will still need some terrestrial infrastructure to use the spectrum for mobile coverage.

## Science & Futuristic Technology

### [The Start-Up That Wants to Build ‘Microrobots' With AI (8 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FsOBLQW/1/010001a120525ad8-f8a5036d-d055-47fa-9623-977563e5eb28-000000/vcU-hSRcTSuEvjOLhIrbyiQH_BiwmFAyiZ6UhrLvAJo=452)
Atomic Machines is building AI technology to discover new ways of designing extremely small devices. It is developing an automated manufacturing system that turns computer code into physical devices to revamp the field of microelectromechanical systems (MEMS). Traditionally manufactured like computer chips, these tiny electrical and mechanical devices are often limited by the physical properties of a semiconductor.
