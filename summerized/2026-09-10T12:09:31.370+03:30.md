# TLDR AI | 2026-09-09

## 🚀 Headlines & Launches

### [ChatGPT Images 2.5](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/ccEMbs)
OpenAI has released ChatGPT Images 2.5, featuring sharper image details, improved preservation of reference images, more reliable editing capabilities, and a 50% reduction in generation latency.

### [OpenAI Model Solved the Navier–Stokes Millennium Problem](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/KhjZoT)
An internal OpenAI system has reportedly produced a proof for the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of mathematics' seven Millennium Prize Problems. The model demonstrated that smooth 3D fluid dynamics can develop a finite-time singularity, providing both an analytical proof and a formalization in Lean.

### [Introducing Muse: Personal AI Agent by Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)
Meta introduced Muse, a personal AI agent designed to automate tasks like travel booking and emailing. It runs on a "Muse Secure VM" with a "Sentinel" agent overseeing actions for data privacy. It is currently available on iOS, Android, and muse.ai in the US.

## 🧠 Deep Dives & Analysis

### [>10x More Efficient Pretraining](https://magic.dev/blog/pretraining)
Magic.dev has developed a pretraining recipe that is 10 times more compute-efficient than leading open-weight models. The startup argues that combining efficient pretraining with agentic reinforcement learning and long-context capabilities is the key to building superhuman coding agents.

### [Inside the Megakernel Serving Engine for North Mini Code](https://cohere.com/blog/megakernels)
Cohere presents a serving system built around a decode megakernel that supports continuous batching and paged attention. It reaches 292 tokens per second, performing 1.58× faster than vLLM while maintaining accuracy across a 256K context window.

### [Pretraining Progress is Mostly Coming from Data](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/pretraining-progress-is-mostly-data)
Analysis of progress between 2019 and 2025 shows that 3.24x more compute efficiency gains have resulted from data improvements rather than model architecture. While model research pushes scaling constraints, data quality remains the primary driver for gains in smaller models.

## 🧑‍💻 Engineering & Research

### [Introducing Mercury 2.5](https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-2-5)
Mercury 2.5 is the largest diffusion language model ever trained. It offers performance comparable to frontier models like Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and Claude Haiku 4.5, outputting 1,107 tokens per second with a 260K-token context window.

### [Hyper-τ-bench: Evaluating Agents that Build Agents](https://sierra.ai/blog/hyper-t-bench-evaluating-agents-that-build-agents)
This new benchmark tests developer agents in sandboxed environments. Results show that while high-reasoning models like Claude Opus 5 pass only 23.9% of tasks alone, they reach 82.2% when paired with an engineer providing deep context.

### [Google's AlphaGenome Maps 9 Billion Genetic Variants](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/alphagenome-atlas/)
Google DeepMind has launched the AlphaGenome Atlas, a 1-petabyte database that predicts the regulatory effects of all 9 billion possible single-nucleotide variants in the human genome.

### [I Asked 100 Agents to Hack Me](https://blog.sshh.io/p/i-asked-100-agents-to-hack-me)
An experiment involving 100 self-hosted agents resulted in five compromised accounts through software vulnerabilities and brute-forcing. The study highlights the security risks posed as open-source models become more capable and cheaper to deploy.

### [Is the 3x AI Productivity Gain just a Computer that Never Sleeps?](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/57ewM6)
Data suggests that AI productivity gains come from machines working parallel, around-the-clock shifts. OpenAI researchers now supervise over 3 "agent-workdays" per 8-hour human shift, though the inference costs for this leverage have risen significantly.
