# TLDR AI 2026-09-08

## 🚀 Headlines & Launches

### [Anthropic signed $517bn in compute agreements in past 11 months](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/anthropic-signed-517bn-in-compute-agreements-in-past-11-months/)
Anthropic has secured $517 billion in compute capacity leases (14.8GW) primarily with Google and AWS. The expansion includes deals with Akamai, Fluidstack, and a $45bn agreement with Nscale. The company filed for a confidential IPO in June.

### [OpenAI prepares managed agents for DevDay 2026](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-prepares-managed-agents-for-devday-2026/)
OpenAI plans to launch Managed Agents at DevDay 2026. Similar to Anthropic's model, these agents will target businesses with advanced computer-use capabilities and competitive pricing, potentially challenging the advertising dominance of Meta and Google through interactive functionality.

## 🧠 Deep Dives & Analysis

### [TPU Inference Externalization Full Steam Ahead](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com/blog/tpu-inferencex-full-steam)
Google's TPUv7 Ironwood reportedly delivers 50% better performance per dollar than Nvidia's B200/B300 chips. Google is now moving to make its TPU software stack more accessible to external developers to compete for inference workloads in the cloud.

### [Prompt Injection Through Tool Output](https://www.armosec.io/blog/untrusted-tool-output-prompt-injection/)
Prompt injections can hide in tool results, bypassing traditional safeguards. Researchers propose using a "precedent gap" signal—detecting when an agent makes a tool call or uses arguments that are absent from its prior execution history—to identify these attacks.

### [Automatically detecting AI text in my browser](https://www.seangoedecke.com/deckard/)
A developer created "Deckard," a Chrome extension that runs a local model to automatically scan and mark AI-generated text on websites, allowing users to avoid AI content while browsing.

### [The Two MMLU Scores: What a Benchmark Name Does Not Fix](https://zatona.dev/blog/the-two-mmlu-scores)
The MMLU benchmark often yields inconsistent results across different providers because of variations in runners, graders, and dataset splits. The "MMLU" label refers to a dataset family rather than a standardized measurement procedure.

## 🧑‍💻 Engineering & Research

### [Google Accelerator Agents for TPU Development](https://github.com/AI-Hypercomputer/accelerator-agents)
This toolkit uses Gemini to help developers migrate PyTorch workloads to JAX and optimize Pallas kernels for Google Cloud TPUs. It includes MaxCode for model conversion and MaxKernel for debugging and profiling.

### [Qwen-Drive](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-Drive-1.0)
A Vision-Language Foundation model designed for autonomous driving. It uses a staged training strategy to integrate perception and planning, maintaining general instruction-following capabilities while specializing in driving tasks.

### [hip-agent: a harness that fits in the prompt](https://jonathanc.net/blog/hip-agent?v=2)
A minimalist agent harness where the core loop is approximately 200 lines of Python. It uses environment variables for configuration and shell commands for actions, relying on standard protocols to keep the footprint small.
