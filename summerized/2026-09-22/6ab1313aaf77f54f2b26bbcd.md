## Engineering and Applications

* **[China builds early sub-3nm GAA transistors using older DUV lithography, sidestepping EUV restrictions (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Finterestingengineering.com%2Finnovation%2Fchina-older-chipmaking-technology-smaller-chips%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a0c4283e28-df2e0061-5eff-4690-8a13-2c9aab97f516-000000/VT3hw-UJ0Dd97qDLd2wRwVowGlpsUjR82SCwN27RDxo=452)**
