## Science & Futuristic Technology

### [Is There Anything a Fruit Fly Brain Can't Do? (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FhpYEEi/1/010001a0a9dcfb01-bf38a4f0-ffd0-4f36-b4b5-ca20a31f76f1-000000/J4jE1Mchek4FhQExVwiUsUtVAz6-qIbyOcftz_UWSME=452)
Researchers at the Janelia Research Campus and Google have published a comprehensive map of the adult male fruit fly's brain. Featuring all 166,000 neurons, the model has been used by engineers and artists to simulate complex tasks like playing Doom, solving a Rubik's Cube, and trading cryptocurrencies.

Agility Robotics has introduced a humanoid robot designed to safely operate near human co-workers. Early access begins in early 2027, with general availability slated for late 2027.
