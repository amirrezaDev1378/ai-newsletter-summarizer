This trend connects to a broader interest in physical books and digital detox, translating social media engagement into real-world foot traffic and public funding support.

### [I audited 4 weeks of my LinkedIn posts. Here's what grew my following (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Fposts%2Fjenna-kamal-9977a38b_4-week-follower-audit-ugcPost-7504088715301040128-tqgY%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0a9f41b47-09b3af4f-0906-47b6-af12-ac644dd06520-000000/vlcx2Cnuh-lUKvZoMyB-FfxJDKuhbWZ4AHovhsq_wpw=452)

A 4-week LinkedIn audit revealed that conversation-starting posts, relatable humor, and provocative takes drove the most follower growth. Shorter copy outperformed long paragraphs. ### [Don't use questions in your titles (1 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fapp.sciencesays.com%2Fp%2Fdon-t-use-questions-in-your-titles%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0a9f41b47-09b3af4f-0906-47b6-af12-ac644dd06520-000000/q7ZXSbsFr1k5hPF6ITtK_teJQW5U3BtIdcQk0hobLcM=452)

A Stanford study found that question-based titles generated 71.6% fewer comments and 12.6% fewer clicks across thousands of analyzed articles and posts. Questions may underperform because they signal uncertainty and offer less immediate information. Marketers should test statement-based headlines instead.

### [AI Overviews and paid search: When to fight, influence, or generate demand (7 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fsearchengineland.com%2Fai-overviews-paid-search-fight-influence-generate-demand-488451%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0a9f41b47-09b3af4f-0906-47b6-af12-ac644dd06520-000000/19sSsKWS7ADhGNQTF5ZCETxXXH8qvXmIBTgl_V0-Pqw=452)

AI Overviews complicate traditional search bidding strategies, requiring marketers to classify search terms by intent:
* **Fight:** High-intent commercial terms where top ad placement still drives direct sales.
* **Influence:** Informational terms where SEO and PPC collaborate to earn brand mentions in AI answers.
* **Generate Demand:** Zero-click opportunities where budgets shift toward channels like YouTube and Demand Gen.
