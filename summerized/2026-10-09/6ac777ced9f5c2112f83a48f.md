## Science & Futuristic Technology

### [Zuckerberg's Biohub Partners With DOE, NIH to Invest $1.8 Billion in Biological Data for AI Models (1 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FAvzMzJ/1/010001a11b2be755-d1aacc49-3b00-43b4-9971-7a7e4d5b5d6b-000000/a3ae0douXtCNxzUU50UBnKFoZNv5HDpKPHE2kQ-ZvXk=452)

Biohub is working with the Department of Energy, the National Institutes of Health, and other partners to invest $1.8 billion in building biological data ready for AI models. The initiative aims to help researchers use AI to discover new ways to prevent and treat diseases. The DOE will invest over $500 million over five years, the NIH will coordinate dataset contributions, and Biohub will work alongside the NIH to standardize datasets for training.

### [In Vienna and Beijing, the First Nuclear Clocks Begin to Tick (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2F08ykNi/1/010001a11b2be755-d1aacc49-3b00-43b4-9971-7a7e4d5b5d6b-000000/d0qg2Q_I4CoBnPslNiiWoNh3gVjIjeyheRcZ_XjqGpA=452)

Two independent teams in Vienna and Beijing have built the first clocks that keep time by counting the oscillations of thorium atomic nuclei. The clocks lose only one second every few million years and have the potential to search for certain kinds of dark matter if a particle passing through the nuclei registers as a wobble in the clock's tick.

## Programming, Design & Data Science

### [Influence without authority (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.gabrielvaldivia.com%2Fnotes%2Finfluence-without-authority%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a11b2be755-d1aacc49-3b00-43b4-9971-7a7e4d5b5d6b-000000/k5ka77FqlTm91cX1U92Lw9W-EHk75pgpqMDZFJqBUzo=452)

Super ICs gain influence by consistently handling unglamorous tasks: improving the work, making stories clear, and helping peers succeed. Authority is a natural side effect of being the person others rely on. Focus on sharpening your craft, packaging thinking so it travels without you, and supporting your team.

### [Anti-Patterns in Software Blogging (12 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Frefactoringenglish.com%2Fblog%2Fanti-patterns-software-blogging%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a11b2be755-d1aacc49-3b00-43b4-9971-7a7e4d5b5d6b-000000/MvwFiecF1b532qzuIrR9Gr3igWXct9HA4jWcUcKKJvc=452)

Readers need a compelling reason early on to continue reading an article. Ensure articles can be read from start to finish without needing to click links or hover for tooltips, avoid framing articles as direct follow-ups to past posts, drop unnecessary formality, and test readability across mobile and low-contrast text settings.
