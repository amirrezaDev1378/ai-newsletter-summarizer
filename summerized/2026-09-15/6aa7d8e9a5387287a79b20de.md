## 🚀 Strategies & Tactics

* **[5 hacks to instantly write more persuasive copy (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fupdate%2Furn:li:activity:7504176931098243072%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a09fa74436-77626bd4-51f1-4c1b-8595-83b83aeb0c68-000000/UJaCd5IkodsI8n5DrbGanvZAJsMP6N7ki29cKb2n5M0=452)**
  Before writing, define the emotion and action the copy should trigger. Spend most of the editing time on the first few lines since they determine whether people keep reading. Describe the audience's pain points with specific, concrete language to show you understand the problem. Write conversationally and cut jargon. Then read the copy aloud to catch awkward sentences and weak sections.

* **[Your playbook for LinkedIn ads that work (13 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.growthunhinged.com%2Fp%2Flinkedin-ad-playbook%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a09fa74436-77626bd4-51f1-4c1b-8595-83b83aeb0c68-000000/5RIAzU0ju-zmxjCgdACDY8Mw69m0I1Qv_mwCzd_CsnY=452)**
  Build LinkedIn ads around the buying journey. Use signals to target companies entering a buying window, then start with thought leadership before introducing case studies and offers. Retarget engaged prospects with native lead forms and qualify them with specific questions. Follow up within 5 minutes while interest is fresh, and use sales outcomes to refine targeting and messaging over time.

* **[9 Successful Media Pitch Examples for Every Type of Campaign (18 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.buzzstream.com%2Fblog%2Fmedia-pitch-examples%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a09fa74436-77626bd4-51f1-4c1b-8595-83b83aeb0c68-000000/E37P2Ti8kNkX0Be7i1sK9z1ykg7ebHyHlxk2Gpkf3Ps=452)**
