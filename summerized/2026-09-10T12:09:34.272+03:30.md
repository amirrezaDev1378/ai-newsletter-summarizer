## Deep Dives & Analysis

### [TPU Inference Externalization Full Steam Ahead](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Finferencex.semianalysis.com%2Fblog%2Ftpu-inferencex-full-steam%3Futm_source=tldrai/1/010001a0813b50cf-005ad1f2-4778-40dd-b38c-bc6e7602f306-000000/QRQWUwonGSTBmcvDhhpf_37Sv26Mnh3nATQ6dDpQn5o=452)
Google's TPUv7 Ironwood claims to deliver up to 50% better performance per dollar compared to Nvidia's B200/B300 chips. This generation marks Google’s push to compete for external inference workloads by allowing chips to be purchased outright or rented via Google Cloud.

### [Prompt Injection Through Tool Output](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.armosec.io%2Fblog%2Funtrusted-tool-output-prompt-injection%2F%3Futm_source=tldrai/1/010001a0813b50cf-005ad1f2-4778-40dd-b38c-bc6e7602f306-000000/GmahB9kV3bKryyELbcSsq4X_TflPuTX49mCHvFdUDaU=452)
Hidden prompt injections in tool results can bypass safeguards because traditional scanners often miss the transition between the agent receiving tool output and taking its next action. The article suggests monitoring for "precedent gaps" where agents make calls without supporting execution history.

### [Automatically detecting AI text in my browser](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.seangoedecke.com%2Fdeckard%2F%3Futm_source=tldrai/1/010001a0813b50cf-005ad1f2-4778-40dd-b38c-bc6e7602f306-000000/_DfLoqK_tPg1hbn7vhKPEDl07rlaAdEB1S_9QL7Jdws=452)

### [The Two MMLU Scores: What a Benchmark Name Does Not Fix](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fzatona.dev%2Fblog%2Fthe-two-mmlu-scores%3Futm_source=tldrai/1/010001a0813b50cf-005ad1f2-4778-40dd-b38c-bc6e7602f306-000000/A6kDaE4Vg3_vgPLErdZb8jNPwfLc2F3j0tL7kA9S-4o=452)
