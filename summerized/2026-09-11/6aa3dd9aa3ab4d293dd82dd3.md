### Big Tech & Startups

*   **[Introducing the Agents API](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/6SUOES):** OpenAI has launched its Agents API in public beta, extending the infrastructure behind Codex to developers. The API helps manage context, coordinate subagents, execute code, work with files, and maintain long-running agent tasks reliably.
*   **[Anthropic Blocks Biological Weapons Research Attempts](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/PCLyui):** Anthropic reports that it successfully disrupted multiple attempts to use its AI models for biological weapons research, noting it erred on the side of caution when handling suspicious or obfuscated queries from blocked regions.
