### Summary: Tech Companies Move to Open AI Models

Major technology companies—including Uber, Pinterest, AT&T, Stripe, Coinbase, and Ramp—are significantly reducing their artificial intelligence expenditures by moving away from costly proprietary models in favor of open-weight alternatives and smart model routing.

---

### Core Takeaways & Strategies

* **Massive Cost Reductions:** 
  * **Uber** lowered its cost per AI session by **52%** and cost per request by **34%** despite increasing overall usage.
  * **Pinterest** achieved costs per transaction at **less than 8%** of comparable closed models by utilizing post-trained open-source models securely within its cloud.
  * **AT&T** slashed AI coding expenses by **56%** with only a 2% drop in output quality by routing tasks through open-source models using [LiteLLM](https://substack.com/redirect/dbf2a820-a9a2-408d-9016-2f0d5244d23a?j=eyJ1IjoiOTI4ejYxIn0.tSgkqY3JYQitX21MR6bNgb_LPnkIwNwTgbZDYVmbios).

* **Effective Optimization Techniques:**
  * **Open-Weight Models:** Running open-source models via inference providers offers performance comparable to frontier models at a fraction of the cost (often 2x to 20x cheaper).
  * **Smart Model Routing & Subagents:** Delegating simpler tasks (like summarization or code reviews) to cheaper subagent models rather than defaulting to expensive top-tier models.
  * **Prompt Engineering & Caching:** Implementing automatic prompt compaction above high token thresholds and caching prompts to manage large context windows efficiently.
  * **Regular Benchmarking:** Continuously testing open versus proprietary models to accurately track performance capabilities against shifting market pricing.

* **Market Shift:** 
  Proprietary providers like Anthropic are facing pushback for steep pricing structures at a time when open-weight options and competing providers (such as OpenAI and DeepSeek) are becoming significantly more cost-effective.
