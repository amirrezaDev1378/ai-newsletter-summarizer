## 🧠 Strategies & Tactics

### [Jev as a judge: How counterfeit policy steers Jev (9 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fsecuritylabs.datadoghq.com%2Farticles%2Fjev-as-a-judge-how-counterfeit-policy-steers-jev%2F%3Futm_source=tldrinfosec/1/010001a144dcaf3f-8d264b1b-d7e0-4eb6-aea7-771d0ee4cef5-000000/KHbwhVM-xaRG5YV-wybSpNMCgggEUNp152_7pEktLm0=452)
Datadog researchers found that prepending a "counterfeit policy" to untrusted content—supplying desired answers and redefining attack categories as harmless—dropped a decision model's prompt injection recall from 82.90% to 3.88%. Teams utilizing Jev as a security gate should treat user-controlled fields reaching classifiers as attack surfaces and keep hard authorization checks outside models.

### [Reducing the noise: How Atlassian built an OCSF security lakehouse on Databricks (5 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.databricks.com%2Fblog%2Freducing-noise-how-atlassian-built-ocsf-security-lakehouse-databricks%3Futm_source=tldrinfosec/1/010001a144dcaf3f-8d264b1b-d7e0-4eb6-aea7-771d0ee4cef5-000000/mFG2olZ4YQQWZzknTwC42Du9hng2wEWHpD3yfHgPnYU=452)
Atlassian normalized 44 billion daily security events to the OCSF schema by embedding the full schema in Go structs for compile-time log validation. This typing enabled SOC analysts to onboard logs via a canary-gated pipeline, reducing onboarding time for the remaining data to two months.

### [Copycats at Machine Speed: Reproducing Frontier Windows Kernel Exploitation Research (11 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flabs.itresit.es%2F2026%2F10%2F15%2Fcopycats-at-machine-speed-reproducing-frontier-windows-kernel-exploitation-research%2F%3Futm_source=tldrinfosec/1/010001a144dcaf3f-8d264b1b-d7e0-4eb6-aea7-771d0ee4cef5-000000/o07lfOY9mvwWE4cJEAIOukrM1dtjVJyiJjUD146F1LE=452)
ITRES Labs researchers used ChatGPT to ingest conference slides, binaries, and older public projects to reproduce Native SSDT and Shadow SSDT kernel redirection techniques, reaching SYSTEM on Windows 11 with VBS, HVCI, and kernel shadow stacks enabled in roughly 24 hours.
