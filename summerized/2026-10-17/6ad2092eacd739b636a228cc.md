## Strategies & Tactics

* **[How to Pitch Local Newsrooms in the US (9 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.buzzstream.com%2Fblog%2Fhow-to-pitch-local-newsrooms%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a14473cf40-fb7e20ea-cc7f-4c94-8437-3a874d39b4ed-000000/BBGTsS63JVjB0PR5s2Gdl7HZZYx7_PmcLSuVhibp3VM=452)**
  Local news pitches are more effective when they target the right journalist, demonstrate a clear community impact, and provide everything needed to produce the story. Local newsrooms have declined by 81% since 2002, making relevant, ready-to-use pitches especially important. Start by reviewing journalists' recent coverage, then contact assignment editors at TV stations or editors at print and digital outlets when appropriate. Tailor pitches to the format: print needs a clear story and supporting data, TV needs visuals and local people to interview, and radio needs an available guest who can speak. Include a timely local angle, relevant data, quotes, visuals, and event details, and send the pitch before the newsroom's morning planning meeting.

* **[Why brands need to fuel a life, not fill a feed (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fbusiness.pinterest.com%2Fen-au%2Fblog%2Fdesign-the-exit%2F%3F_sp=7b571d78-3f73-49e3-ad3e-a4deb121e1f1.1792018446862%26utm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a14473cf40-fb7e20ea-cc7f-4c94-8437-3a874d39b4ed-000000/VvG65-Q3RrEQHpD7yIRsVUldETjYfQjPua2rZzUTUbw=452)**
  Pinterest reports that 40% of Gen Z wish social media had never been invented and 63% intentionally unplug. Design campaigns that drive real-world action rather than endless scrolling. Show how a product fits into the audience's desired lifestyle instead of making the product the hero. Turn inspiration into action with personalized recommendations and specific next steps such as shopping lists or linked outfits. Remove friction with tools that help people follow through.

* **[The Art of Brand Merch (8 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ffatemaraja.substack.com%2Fp%2Fthe-art-of-brand-merch%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a14473cf40-fb7e20ea-cc7f-4c94-8437-3a874d39b4ed-000000/82q_jNw8MZgMxGl_lN3077K49WS2VDzPKsdaAu3Q3Ek=452)**
  People wear merch to say something about themselves, not to promote a company. A logo on a T-shirt or water bottle asks customers to carry a message without giving them a reason, and only works when the logo already means something. Strong merch does one of three jobs. It signals belonging through a recognizable color or shape, sells more product by being designed around how that product gets used, or lets in people who can't yet afford the core product.

* **[A Standing Holdout Is the Only Audit an Algorithm Cannot Argue With (7 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fkarandhir.substack.com%2Fp%2Fa-standing-holdout-is-the-only-audit%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a14473cf40-fb7e20ea-cc7f-4c94-8437-3a874d39b4ed-000000/88mjSWZ0aAXguam6aI_q2ckF0dbO9eAU-wQ3QF7cy3I=452)**
  Standing holdouts are a measurement method that reserves a portion of markets or audiences from automated ad campaigns to measure whether advertising generates incremental sales. Uber cut $100 million of its $150 million annual rider acquisition budget without reducing app installs, while Airbnb recovered to 95% of its 2019 traffic levels before resuming marketing spend. A standing holdout reserves 5% to 10% of markets and rotates them on a fixed schedule to measure advertising performance over time. Each test defines its markets, measurement period, success thresholds, and budget decisions in advance. If holdout results conflict with the platform's reported returns beyond a predefined margin, the holdout results determine budget decisions.

* **[AI agents buy through your own checkout, and disputes still land on you (8 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fokaneland.com%2Fstudy%2Fcan-an-ai-agent-buy-from-you%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a14473cf40-fb7e20ea-cc7f-4c94-8437-3a874d39b4ed-000000/I0SZoEdQnWcIruaS5YQKcqzrM-qTlIEbL1q0IJj3J28=452)**

Independent sellers instead meet agents like Amazon's Buy for Me and Meta's Muse on their own checkout pages, where guest purchase and saved-wallet support decide whether an order completes. Visa bars merchants from requiring dispute waivers on agent purchases, so chargebacks still land on the seller.
