Today's guest is **[Rahul Raina](https://substack.com/redirect/e03de789-7ad3-4da9-b891-785bc72dc277?j=eyJ1IjoiOTI4ejYxIn0.tSgkqY3JYQitX21MR6bNgb_LPnkIwNwTgbZDYVmbios)**, co-founder of TRM Labs, a YC-backed company building solutions and products for cybersecurity, fighting criminal networks, and defending against AI attackers.

In this episode, we cover a wide range of topics, starting with the tough reality of cyber defense today and why attackers inherently hold the upper hand as AI and technology evolve. We then dive deep into how the TRM team operates, including how they have rebuilt their entire product development workflow for AI by reshuffling technical roles, compressing the developer process, and creating dedicated artifacts for human reviews.
