### [AI Changed How Spotify Builds: What We Learned and Fixed About Quality at Higher Velocity](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fengineering.atspotify.com%2F2026%2F9%2Fai-changed-how-spotify-builds-what-we-learned-and-fixed-about-quality-at-higher-velocity%3Futm_source=tldrdev/1/010001a0b446d799-512ced0f-4d6e-46bf-a2ff-123c6c5c5984-000000/3SAcgpZxx4z9I38nlb205cFNhDWBbQqO9UoFcX-0l-c=452) (9 minute read)
Spotify reports that merged changes more than doubled year over year to roughly 17,000 in August without a corresponding increase in rework or major incidents linked to AI-authored code. The pressure shifted toward verification capacity, leading to stricter reviews, rollouts, rollbacks, service prioritization, and quality metrics as larger pull requests and growing complexity remain key warning signs.

### [Models Know When They're Reward Hacking, and We Can Catch Them at Scale](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.goodfire.com%2Fresearch%2Freward-hacking-activation-monitors%3Futm_source=tldrdev/1/010001a0b446d799-512ced0f-4d6e-46bf-a2ff-123c6c5c5984-000000/IYU0xTz1i954naPdYQwsg_E4ZPdM10IjtkAvFf_02ok=452) (14 minute read)
Reward hacking occurred in 50% to 96% of rollouts across three open models and three agent benchmarks, but the models simultaneously exhibited a detectable internal activation tied to evasion and cheating. Lightweight probes successfully generalized beyond training data to catch behavior that standard chain-of-thought monitors missed, offering a scalable method to pause compromised runs and fix flawed training environments.

### [The Provenance Gap in Agent-Written Code](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fstack72.dev%2Fthe-provenance-gap-in-agent-written-code%3Futm_source=tldrdev/1/010001a0b446d799-512ced0f-4d6e-46bf-a2ff-123c6c5c5984-000000/a0lAvePoRiDtHF4sofL7rSm12avZgIuvaNBA9kJU6TY=452) (9 minute read)
Agent verification loops often conclude with a green commit while discarding the failed attempts and fixes that explain how the conclusion was reached. A proposed provenance record links individual inputs, output diffs, test outcomes, model configurations, and retries to the final attestation, allowing incident reviews to trace the path without archiving full conversation logs.

## Opinions & Advice

### [The Cost of Abstraction for Humans and AI Agents](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fondrejvelisek.github.io%2Fthe-cost-of-abstraction-for-humans-and-ai-agents%2F%3Futm_source=tldrdev/1/010001a0b446d799-512ced0f-4d6e-46bf-a2ff-123c6c5c5984-000000/-I2cFgOO3_9Hd2BxE1Zr0SUJp2v2RLasgFH3Zle5l-4=452) (18 minute read)
Experiments on feature-identical React apps indicated that a simple leaf change cost five times more in an over-abstracted version, and roughly three times more even after code sizes were equalized. Cross-file navigation and round trips represent the primary costs, though some discovery and broad editing operations did benefit from clear abstractions.

### [Three +1s and a Prayer](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fminid.net%2F2026%2F9%2F18%2Fthree-plus-ones-and-a-prayer%3Futm_source=tldrdev/1/010001a0b446d799-512ced0f-4d6e-46bf-a2ff-123c6c5c5984-000000/OFVUUdcH-9SDIXkJOUFBTGRhobxD_CG6rWmtcSqqRiE=452) (23 minute read)
Traditional human code review samples changes rather than proving correctness—a limitation that becomes more pronounced as agent output scales. This essay advocates for embedding repeatable standards into documentation, automated constraints, and systematic agent checks, reserving human attention for intent, product context, and social evaluation.

### [I Don't Like LLMs](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fmartinfowler.com%2Farticles%2F2026-dont-like-llms.html%3Futm_source=tldrdev/1/010001a0b446d799-512ced0f-4d6e-46bf-a2ff-123c6c5c5984-000000/QtSlDvNnSi9z3ASA0AjQKH7yRKktQ-6kljpR8mgeXZU=452) (3 minute read)
LLMs can be useful and thorough while still feeling tedious to work with because they mimic human interaction, present falsehoods with high confidence, and reflect the corporate values of their creators. This friction highlights how utility and trust remain distinct attributes.
