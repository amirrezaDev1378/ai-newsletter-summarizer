## Engineering and Applications

* **[Onsemi replaces tiny wires inside power chips to cut energy losses (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.servethehome.com%2Fonsemi-embedded-power-platform-announced%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a0b4aeee5c-bdac12df-d192-4758-9ecf-597952ca558d-000000/7FrOaU7x6_4mR_65NPe9DB4aDSCa3Z-BhAro0DH9B0M=452)**  
  Onsemi's new Embedded Power Platform (EPP) builds power components directly into silicon and connects them with etched layers instead of wire bonds, reducing electrical losses and enabling faster switching speeds.

* **[Nvidia graphics chips contain up to 40 tiny processors for housekeeping (5 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.xda-developers.com%2Fyour-nvidia-gpu-dozens-risc-v-cores-one-took-over-graphics-driver%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a0b4aeee5c-bdac12df-d192-4758-9ecf-597952ca558d-000000/ByPzuBrGRbEEv6o6Otsv8lU3ais0Bz2yFRNqCYw0Hxs=452)**  
  Nvidia GPUs utilize between 10 and 40 RISC-V management processors to handle housekeeping tasks like clocking, memory, and security. These cores are cryptographically locked down and inaccessible to users.
