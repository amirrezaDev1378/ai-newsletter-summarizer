### [Is 3rd party data the biggest lever in GEO? Our data says no (2 minute read)](https://www.growandconvert.com/research/third-party-citation-research/?utm_source=tldrmarketing)

Third-party pages make up 85% of AI citations, but they are not the strongest predictor of whether a brand gets mentioned. Of the answers that mentioned the brand, 87% cited its own site, while third-party pages featuring it were cited far less often. When the brand's site was cited, it was mentioned 90% of the time in Google AI Overviews and 73% in ChatGPT.

## Strategies & Tactics

### [Cheapest LinkedIn Ads retargeting source (1 minute read)](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7511411498196324352/?utm_source=tldrmarketing)

A low-cost way to build a LinkedIn retargeting audience is to run 60-70 second videos with a Website Visits objective. Use a headline that discourages clicks because advertisers only pay when someone clicks, and anyone who watches at least 50% of the video is added to the retargeting audience. This tactic builds audiences for about $0.50 per member versus $1-$2 per click for standard engagement campaigns.

### [An Event-Led Community Strategy Guide (6 minute read)](https://www.standingongiants.com/blog/how-to-host-community-events-an-event-led-community-strategy-guide/?utm_source=tldrmarketing)

Community events work best as part of an ongoing community strategy rather than one-off activations. Assess whether the community is ready for in-person interaction, then design smaller peer-led formats that prioritize conversation over production value. Plan follow-ups before the event with a dedicated conversation channel to help turn attendees into active members.

### [Why you're worth it (8 minute read)](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/why-youre-worth-it-copywriting-friday/?utm_source=tldrmarketing)

Furniture brand Vitsœ uses its pricing page to justify why its products cost more than cheaper alternatives by breaking costs into smaller time-based amounts and pointing to resale prices as evidence of durability. It avoids sales and financing options that could boost short-term revenue, making its pricing claims more credible.

## Miscellaneous

### [How to price and package for D2C without breaking your game economy (5 minute read)](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/in-game-vs-web-store-how-to-price-and-package-for-d2c-without-breaking-your-game-economy/?utm_source=tldrmarketing)

Only 2-3% of players spend money in mobile games, making the first purchase difficult when it requires leaving the app. Publishers should assess the game economy before setting D2C offers—extra entitlements can outperform discounts by preserving revenue per purchase while adding value. Direct checkout, creator codes, loyalty currencies, and exclusive web content can also drive purchases without lowering prices.

### [Can You SEO Your Way Into an AI Agent's Recommendation? (7 minute read)](https://www.joe-shirey.com/2026/10/02/seo-for-ai-agents.html?utm_source=tldrmarketing)

AI agents recommend products mostly from what they learned in training rather than real-time search returns. On advisory questions, agents rarely search unless prompted, and search behavior depends heavily on the prompt and tools set by the operator. Even when every search was forced and queries were rewritten to favor one vendor, that vendor was selected only 17.1% of the time, suggesting SEO has limited short-term influence on recommendations.
