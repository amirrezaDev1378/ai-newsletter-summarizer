## Programming, Design & Data Science

### [e2e (Website)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ftester.army%2Fe2e%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a10bb9b339-8dba8203-199f-4eba-868d-ca89b67498e1-000000/1txc3wXamtK5KVY8GkEMOjgTPeGdlTwyOrJvQfaE05g=452)

e2e is an open-source AI testing framework providing customizable testing for web and mobile apps. By remembering agent actions and replaying them when possible without recalling the model, it reduces repeated reasoning, lowers token usage, and cuts costs while letting developers choose their preferred AI model.
