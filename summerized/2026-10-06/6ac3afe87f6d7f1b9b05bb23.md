## 🚀 Headlines & Launches

### [Prime Inference: Fast, Reliable Serving for Frontier Open Models (18 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.primeintellect.ai%2Fblog%2Fprime-inference%3Futm_source=tldrai/1/010001a10c671225-db827b6b-a15c-4afd-9a6b-cb61658ad843-000000/xAJh0brvQ1y5JzK7DehHgKD6o3Ze_6z-JbnFtKxwCrA=452)
Prime Inference covers both serverless endpoints and reserved capacity. It offers resilient serving of frontier open-source models on Prime Intellect's GPU infrastructure across multiple data centers. The serving platform has powered large-scale RL rollouts, synthetic data generation, evaluations, and long-running coding agents at Prime, processing nearly a trillion tokens internally every day.

### [Aleph Alpha releases open-weight Kolibri with 1M context (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.testingcatalog.com%2Faleph-alpha-releases-open-weight-kolibri-with-1m-context%2F%3Futm_source=tldrai/1/010001a10c671225-db827b6b-a15c-4afd-9a6b-cb61658ad843-000000/AdNXBwrd3UaaCPacjTVdXhwEj1WpR6VoVl3yCT5qDco=452)
Kolibri is an open-weight English-German Mixture-of-Experts model with 78.1 billion parameters. It supports tool calling and contexts up to one million tokens and is available on Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 license for sovereign, mission-critical work in government and regulated industries.

### [Anthropic to invest $100 million to train AI engineer talent (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.cnbc.com%2F2026%2F10%2F02%2Fanthropic-to-invest-100-million-to-train-ai-engineer-talent.html%3Futm_source=tldrai/1/010001a10c671225-db827b6b-a15c-4afd-9a6b-cb61658ad843-000000/ZB52lVoVCKPV8Y5KIqj3SjPIkvPf20aLXSNB69NEb6s=452)
