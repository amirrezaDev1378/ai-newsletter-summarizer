## Research and Prototypes

* **[LiMA Splits Robot "Thinking" From "Reacting" Into Two Async Systems, Cuts Latency 45.8% (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farxiv.org%2Fabs%2F2609.28431%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a0d8bd3363-5dfa4b92-e86a-4260-b1ef-f6ff29386f51-000000/iaQTjVa-dWPm8CEq-OKr4dkBYV2a9Ywb5puQToIsPGQ=452)**
  LiMA decouples dexterous manipulation into an asynchronous dual-system hierarchy: a slow system for long-horizon intent and a fast system for high-frequency motion, linked by a Latent Schrödinger Bridge Coupling mechanism. This reduces inference latency by 45.8% compared to Cosmos-Policy and achieved a 70.8% success rate across six bimanual tasks.

* **[Chip Design's Real AI Bottleneck Isn't Individual Tools, It's What Happens Between Them (7 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fsemiengineering.com%2Fedas-future-is-evidence-driven-automation%2F%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a0d8bd3363-5dfa4b92-e86a-4260-b1ef-f6ff29386f51-000000/gNzOAQNgO0dR5PTMC2YaIE5blYmFqvx1lt2Bbj6n4z8=452)**
  Semiconductor design suffers from intent-transfer failures during handoffs between specialized tools (RTL, synthesis, verification). The emerging consensus is "evidence-driven automation," where AI proposes fixes and orchestrates handoffs, but outputs remain independently checkable through formal proof and deterministic verification.
