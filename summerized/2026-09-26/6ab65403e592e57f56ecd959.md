## Big Tech & Startups

### [Google's first Suncatcher orbital data center test launches October 1 (3 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/09/googles-first-suncatcher-orbital-data-center-test-launches-october-1/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Project Suncatcher is a moonshot effort by Google to design orbital AI data centers, with its first experimental satellite scheduled to launch on October 1. The mission aims to validate Google's vision for a constellation of AI satellites. The refrigerator-sized satellite features four custom TPU AI accelerators and solar panels that supply approximately one kilowatt of power, serving as a technology testbed over its few months of operation.

### [Jeff Bezos' Blue Origin Is Fueled With $30 Billion of His Fortune (4 minute read)](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/Nv2kGO)

Jeff Bezos has invested $30 billion in Blue Origin since its founding in 2000, helping the space company scale to around 15,000 employees. Blue Origin recently raised capital from outside investors for the first time and expects to produce about $1.4 billion in revenue this year, with projections to grow that number to over $30 billion by 2030.

### [Waymo is scaling fast: Here's what the fleet data shows (4 minute read)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/waymo-is-scaling-fast-heres-what-the-fleet-data-shows/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Waymo is concentrating its scaling efforts heavily in California and Texas, which house 80% of its roughly 4,000 robotaxis. ### [There's a new way to break RSA that's faster than anything we've seen before (5 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/theres-a-new-way-to-break-rsa-thats-faster-than-anything-weve-seen-before/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Researchers have discovered a novel classical computing method involving signature forgery that reduces the current RSA security level to an unacceptably low threshold while reducing required computing resources by orders of magnitude. While the practical risk is limited and widely used RSA implementations remain safe, the technique is faster than previously seen methods.

## Programming, Design & Data Science

### [A new trend of CPU shortages (10 minute read)](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/the-pulse-a-new-trend-of-cpu-shortages/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Companies are facing widespread CPU shortages due to a lack of spot pricing amid high demand. Securing CPUs now requires long-running connections with cloud providers and months-ahead reservations, and cloud providers are increasingly turning down reservations due to insufficient inventory or wrong CPU types.

### [Thinking in Systems, Shipping in Loops (3 minute read)](https://tomtunguz.com/thinking-in-systems%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter)

AI has shifted the engineering burden from typing code to system design and building loops that verify output. Effective system design relies on resilience (AI checking its own work), self-organization (the learning loop), and hierarchy (composible, reused layers of skills, tools, and sandboxes).
