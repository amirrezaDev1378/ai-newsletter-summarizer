This dynamic plays into the hands of stakeholders dubbed "Fastest Guns in the West," who use speed and deliberate ambiguity to control product narratives and secure early commitment to ideas before proper evaluation can occur.

### [Has your product outgrown its floor plan? (6 minute read)](https://yeseniaperezcruz.substack.com/p/has-your-product-outgrown-its-floor?utm_source=tldrproduct)
Successful digital products often outgrow their initial structural simplicity as features accumulate, requiring a clear spatial "floor plan" rather than just consistent design system components. Mismatched spatial movement and flat UI cues make products harder for users to navigate and understand.
