* **[Building a GPU compiler from scratch, from PyTorch to CUDA (10 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fkernelspace.substack.com%2Fp%2Fbuilding-a-gpu-compiler-from-scratch%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a13a959dd5-10eb098b-6b74-4aa5-9576-24e12d7f3b9c-000000/OXHrbCb4m-_7gXPV1pl9H44YwfH8rGd1Np1lfyIwxLs=452)**

## Research and Prototypes

* **[Apple's Minions pools idle Macs into a virtual GPU cloud (10 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farxiv.org%2Fabs%2F2610.12970%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a13a959dd5-10eb098b-6b74-4aa5-9576-24e12d7f3b9c-000000/1Jx6x6mGJ1yk3kKZC8EUmhsdVUPqUUeSGZGkPz2RhSU=452)**
  Apple Silicon Macs have powerful GPUs that sit mostly idle in normal use. Minions combines them across machines into a virtual GPU cloud for large ML inference jobs, with fault tolerance and flexible task scheduling. The pitch is that teams can use hardware they already own instead of waiting for expensive, centralized GPU capacity.

* **[Anthropic runs a 26B model across 15 $55 TV boxes to test IoT self-replication (5 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.anthropic.com%2Fresearch%2Fself-replicating-ai%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a13a959dd5-10eb098b-6b74-4aa5-9576-24e12d7f3b9c-000000/mhjOZZTLLJUswQuYOlSAG4wuIwGIQlIBwmQikHRf-TY=452)**
  Anthropic's Frontier Red Team ran Gemma4-26B-A4B across 15 off-brand Android TV boxes with 4GB of RAM each, using pipeline parallelism at 0.39 tokens per second, to test whether small models could replicate across cheap IoT hardware. A 9B model got inference running on 8 of 9 hardware types tested, and a Kimwolf-sized botnet would total only about 40 H100s of compute. By their trend line, small models reach the needed capability in simplified tests by year-end.
