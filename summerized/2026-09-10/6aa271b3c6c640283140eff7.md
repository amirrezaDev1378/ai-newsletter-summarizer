## Overview & Background

Roman Ugarte helped incubate and build **Grok Bot**, a knowledge-work AI agent developed at SpaceXAI. A small, isolated team moved from writing the first line of code to a working internal product in four weeks, followed by a public launch just three weeks later. Prior to Grok Bot, Roman led Growth at Cursor, scaling the team from 15 people to over 1,000 before its acquisition by SpaceX.

---

## Core Topics & Key Takeaways

* **Building Standalone vs. Integrating:** The strategic decision to build Grok Bot from scratch rather than embedding it as an addition to existing products like Cursor.
* **High-Touch Early Onboarding:** The rationale behind personally onboarding nearly 300 initial users to gather direct qualitative insights and refine UX.
* **Early Critical Product Choices:** Two foundational product decisions that set Grok Bot apart early in development.
* **"Colleague-Pilled" Product Philosophy:** Designing AI agents to function and feel more like active collaborators rather than simple static software tools.
* **Moats in Competitive AI Markets:** Lessons on maintaining speed, focus, and product-led differentiation in highly competitive markets.
