## 🚀 Headlines & Launches

### [ChatGPT Images 2.5](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/ccEMbs)
OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Images 2.5 with sharper details, better reference-image preservation, more reliable editing, and up to 50% lower generation latency.

### [An OpenAI Model Solved the Navier–Stokes Millennium Problem](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/KhjZoT)
OpenAI announced that an internal AI system produced a proof resolving the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of mathematics' seven Millennium Prize Problems. The model produced both an analytical proof and a Lean formalization, showing that smooth three-dimensional fluid dynamics can develop a finite-time singularity.

### [Introducing Muse: The World's First Personal AI Agent Built for Everyone](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/?utm_source=tldrai)
Meta has introduced Muse, a personal AI agent designed to automate tasks like booking travel and sending emails. It operates on a secure VM to ensure data privacy and is available on iOS, Android, and muse.ai in the US.

## 🧠 Deep Dives & Analysis

### [>10x More Efficient Pretraining](https://magic.dev/blog/pretraining?utm_source=tldrai)
Magic has developed a pretraining recipe that is 10 times more compute-efficient than leading open-weight models. The startup argues that pretraining, agentic RL, and long-context are the keys to building superhuman coding agents.

### [Inside the megakernel serving engine for North Mini Code](https://cohere.com/blog/megakernels?utm_source=tldrai)
Cohere presents a serving system built around a decode megakernel that supports continuous batching and paged attention. It reaches 292 tokens per second on batch size 1, performing 1.58× faster than vLLM with no loss of accuracy.

### [Pretraining progress is mostly coming from data](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/pretraining-progress-is-mostly-data?utm_source=tldrai)
Analysis shows that between 2019 and 2025, 3.24x more compute efficiency gains came from data improvements rather than model architecture. While small models see massive gains from data quality, these improvements may scale differently for larger models.

## 🧑‍💻 Engineering & Research

### [Introducing Mercury 2.5](https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-2-5?utm_source=tldrai)
Mercury 2.5 is the largest diffusion language model ever trained. It offers performance comparable to frontier models like GPT-5.6 Luna, outputs 1,107 tokens per second, and features a 260K-token context window.

### [Hyper-τ-bench: Evaluating agents that build agents](https://sierra.ai/blog/hyper-t-bench-evaluating-agents-that-build-agents?utm_source=tldrai)
This new benchmark evaluates developer agents tasked with building customer-service agents from scratch. Research shows that while top-tier models like Claude Opus 5 pass 23.9% of tasks alone, they reach 82.2% when paired with an engineer with deep context.

### [Google's AlphaGenome Maps 9 Billion Genetic Variants](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/alphagenome-atlas/?utm_source=tldrai)
Google DeepMind introduced AlphaGenome Atlas, a 1-petabyte database predicting the regulatory effects of all 9 billion possible single-nucleotide variants in the human genome.
