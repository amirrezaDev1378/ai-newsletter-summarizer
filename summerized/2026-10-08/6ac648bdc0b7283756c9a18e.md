## Engineering and Applications

### [Synopsys and OpenAI team up on GPT-Synopsys, a frontier model that runs EDA tools like an engineer (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.eetimes.com%2Fgpt-synopsys-combines-ic-design-eda-with-agentic-ai%2F%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a1168c1336-b7a63720-dc1e-405c-bb2a-8909fce06818-000000/Oru3OnyE53S_P7gHjGQ6C4HRsWOS-Y0z9gU2Pbdupeo=452)

GPT-Synopsys is a new OpenAI frontier model tuned to operate Synopsys' Electronic Design Automation (EDA) tools for tasks such as PPA optimization, timing closure, and verification. Engineers provide an objective, and autonomous agents handle the tools, interpret results, apply adjustments, and iterate until a verified outcome is reached. The bundled service covers compute, the model, and EDA licenses, with a revenue-sharing structure tied to design improvements. Synopsys confirmed that customer design data is protected and will not be used for training the model.

### [Designing chips for extreme environments (12 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fsemiengineering.com%2Fextreme-environments-push-chips-to-their-breaking-point%2F%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a1168c1336-b7a63720-dc1e-405c-bb2a-8909fce06818-000000/QFDs8rHwp10jsZfkDw5pzIkx9_PUXgh-tZ-MYHRjVHQ=452)

Engineers are shifting toward testing chips against combined stresses—such as radiation, thermal shock, and vibration simultaneously—rather than qualifying conditions individually. Arizona State University has paired a radiation source with a cryostat to evaluate devices at 77 Kelvin. While digital twins help model system behavior, physical testing remains essential for validating predictions.

## Research and Prototypes

### [RoboParty demonstrates the RP1 humanoid (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Finterestingengineering.com%2Fai-robotics%2Frobopartys-rp1-open-source-humanoid-robotics%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a1168c1336-b7a63720-dc1e-405c-bb2a-8909fce06818-000000/-YRp8qwQC6VGINUPX8RenZhxYZSz8XpmTn7wLogqH28=452)

RoboParty showcased the RP1 humanoid robot at IROS, demonstrating its ability to recover balance after being pushed or kicked. The robot integrates custom actuator modules, specialized hardware, and reinforcement-learning motion control with peak joint torque reaching up to 160 Nm. RoboParty plans to open-source additional control software, simulation tools, and hardware designs over time.

### [ScaRF-SLAM: Scale-Consistent Reconstruction with Feed-Forward Models and Classical Visual SLAM (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fdynamic.robots.ox.ac.uk%2Fprojects%2Fscarf-slam%2F%3Futm_source=tldrhardware/1/010001a1168c1336-b7a63720-dc1e-405c-bb2a-8909fce06818-000000/lUj6jj-w6rpk8kMIjzatwKYSVhzB89b8pgnUxhWc2sU=452)

ScaRF-SLAM is a dense visual mapping system combining classical visual SLAM reliability with feed-forward geometric foundation models for 3D reconstruction. By decoupling camera tracking from dense depth estimation and enforcing scale consistency across submaps, the system builds globally accurate metric-scale maps in real-time across diverse sensor arrays without sacrificing tracking stability.
