### [World's first enhanced geothermal power plant completed in just 23 months (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F10%2F01%2Fworlds-first-enhanced-geothermal-power-plant-completed-in-just-23-months%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a0fc439e8f-0be5f6c8-2455-4651-84aa-f0b76f3b7e44-000000/oieARgGytgNUUsLH-FId_oK8Nw6V_x_NXWC4_5Csf-s=452)

Fervo Energy has started selling electricity from its Cape Station geothermal power plant. It is the first enhanced geothermal company to reach a key commercial milestone. The entire site has the potential to generate as much as 4 gigawatts of electricity. The first block at the site took 23 months to complete - Fervo aims to complete future blocks in as little as 18 months.

### [With PRIMA, NASA will try to build a billion-dollar space telescope in record time (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farstechnica.com%2Fspace%2F2026%2F09%2Fnasa-green-lights-billion-dollar-infrared-observatory-for-launch-in-early-2030s%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a0fc439e8f-0be5f6c8-2455-4651-84aa-f0b76f3b7e44-000000/Y38BR_ejG-7AAThez_T2cfCIPVpx1ZhatA5HQyfgE1Y=452)

NASA's Probe Explorers are a new line of observatories intended to do more science for less money. The project aims to seek a better balance between the smaller Explorer missions and multibillion-dollar flagships. NASA announced on Wednesday it was moving forward with the first of the Explorers, PRIMA (Probe far-Infrared Mission for Astrophysics). PRIMA will observe the universe in faint, far-infrared light in a range of wavelengths between 24 and 235 micrometers. NASA intends to launch the telescope in 2033.

### [Pi Durable (33 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fearendil.com%2Fposts%2Fpi-durable%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a0fc439e8f-0be5f6c8-2455-4651-84aa-f0b76f3b7e44-000000/N7L_iZYAEZhwPU_DDYywUxkugAfnI9vnj8m7j2r41tE=452)
