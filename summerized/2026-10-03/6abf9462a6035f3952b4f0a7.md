## News & Trends

### [Building a post-quantum certificate authority with Merkle Tree Certificates (10 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fblog.cloudflare.com%2Fpq-ca-with-mtcs%2F%3Futm_source=tldrdevops/1/010001a0fc5b89e1-719321d6-e612-4c8c-8890-546671e52468-000000/AAovNJciFlq8z6c6AMpuNFh_qy6REc0G3mYjfq4N7bU=452)
Cloudflare is launching a certificate authority that will support Merkle Tree Certificates (MTCs), targeting early 2027 for inclusion in Chrome's Quantum-resistant Root Store. The company will provide standard MTC issuance at no cost. MTCs batch certificates into an append-only Merkle tree to address the scaling issues of post-quantum cryptography, which can increase log storage requirements by 40 times. Cloudflare successfully tested the technology with Chrome, serving billions of MTCs and finding that median performance was 9% faster than classical signature chains.

### [Amazon S3 Vectors now supports metadata pre-filtering for higher recall on filtered searches (5 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Faws.amazon.com%2Fblogs%2Faws%2Famazon-s3-vectors-now-supports-metadata-pre-filtering-for-higher-recall-on-filtered-searches%2F%3Futm_source=tldrdevops/1/010001a0fc5b89e1-719321d6-e612-4c8c-8890-546671e52468-000000/XC5LrxNoZVGLMTQzOn5c3LzzHAJhzK8p919Hcz6wGjU=452)
AWS has introduced metadata pre-filtering for Amazon S3 Vectors to improve recall on filtered queries. The feature evaluates metadata filters before running a similarity search and supports up to 100 filter constraints per query. On highly selective filters, pre-filtering returns up to five times more matching vectors than previous methods. It is available immediately at no additional cost and works on existing indexes without requiring data re-ingestion.

### [Cloudflare Containers, rebuilt to scale agent sandboxes (15 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fblog.cloudflare.com%2Ffaster-agent-sandboxes%2F%3Futm_source=tldrdevops/1/010001a0fc5b89e1-719321d6-e612-4c8c-8890-546671e52468-000000/mkqGWVsGUELWstAyFj_zFT4zwsIv1AvMK0GJa4GCjOo=452)

A filesystem snapshots feature is also available in public beta to let workspaces save and restore their state.
