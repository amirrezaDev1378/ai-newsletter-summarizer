## News & Trends

### [AI Shopping Could Mean Fewer Returns (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.practicalecommerce.com%2Fai-shopping-could-mean-fewer-returns%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0fc59a301-ceb29aeb-6b6c-4ae6-ade7-f84d567bdb42-000000/nTTrdc4GsTklE-KUu8bIcT_2jA5pkHCWTEC8r8jXqHc=452)
AI shopping can reduce ecommerce returns by resolving uncertainty prior to purchase. Adobe data indicates that 69% of consumers using AI for shopping are less likely to return AI-assisted purchases, and AI-referred traffic converts 60% higher than non-AI traffic. Merchants can enhance these results by supplying detailed product specifications, dimensions, variants, compatibility details, and FAQs to help AI accurately recommend products.

### [Marketers Are Rebranding as ‘Engineers' to Survive the AI Era (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FPjHhyg/1/010001a0fc59a301-ceb29aeb-6b6c-4ae6-ade7-f84d567bdb42-000000/llDKNbt8gtyxFcI_ac79R5G4Z9wGrwNfruKBHcyUtPE=452)
The "marketing engineer" combines builder and marketer roles, deploying AI agents to automate tasks like media buying while providing human taste. Companies like Profound are leaning heavily into this title via job boards, hackathons, and podcasts, though skeptics view it as a rebranded buzzword attempting to borrow engineering prestige.

## Strategies & Tactics

### [How Brands Are Fighting AI Slop (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fthebrandblueprint.substack.com%2Fp%2Fhow-brands-are-fighting-ai-slop%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0fc59a301-ceb29aeb-6b6c-4ae6-ade7-f84d567bdb42-000000/FqkpAusuSh_cTjka3qgxLG3oGE-BtRSU5k29UqWpQkc=452)
Brands are increasingly leaning on human and physical signals to prove content is not machine-made. J.Crew's Barn Jacket campaign utilized 160 real people across five cities for cultural context that AI cannot replicate. Other strategies include commissioning artists, exposing messy behind-the-scenes processes, and investing in print and physical retail.

### [Reddit Moderator Outreach: How to Build Real Relationships With Subreddit Mods (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.standingongiants.com%2Fblog%2Freddit-moderator-outreach%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0fc59a301-ceb29aeb-6b6c-4ae6-ade7-f84d567bdb42-000000/UeGxWRVeVcSeyQdi5DQz5zXdfAG5Yli6C769MuVSH8U=452)
Effective Reddit outreach starts with deep research into community rules and culture. Using a named, actively engaged account, marketers should introduce themselves clearly, state how they can contribute value (e.g., expert answers or early access), and follow rules without asking for promotional permissions upfront. Consistent, long-term participation can convert moderators into trusted partners.
