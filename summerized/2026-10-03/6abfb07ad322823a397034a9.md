## Engineering and Applications

* **[Crossing Chiplet Boundaries With PCIe Over UCIe (5 minute read)](https://semiengineering.com/crossing-chiplet-boundaries-with-pcie-over-ucie/?utm_source=tldrhardware)**
  PCIe 8.0 traffic can cross chiplet boundaries through UCIe 3.0, though engineers must carefully coordinate power, reset, and recovery states to avoid timing mismatches, completion timeouts, and deadlocks.

* **[Memory At The Edge: Non-Volatile Memory Challenges And Requirements For Humanoid Robots (8 minute read)](https://semiengineering.com/memory-at-the-edge-non-volatile-memory-challenges-and-requirements-for-humanoid-robots/?utm_source=tldrhardware)**
  Humanoid robots require distinct memory architectures for centralized AI compute versus distributed joint control. While Flash and EEPROM can delay writes by 5–10 milliseconds, alternatives like F-RAM and NOR Flash can handle continuous telemetry, boot storage, and time-sensitive networking safely.
