### [Advertising is Going AI. TV Isn't (3 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fmikeshields.substack.com%2Fp%2Fadvertising-is-going-ai-tv-isnt%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0d324cd8b-f1aefd5a-1b56-4072-9ac9-a95416a43e45-000000/a57HR1ZdVclFmw5ttO0U22yFihpsIA_3CActJof7HwU=452)

Complex buying workflows and fragmented inventory make automation harder, though AI could simplify TV buying if backed by strong first-party data.

### [More lower-income households feel financially vulnerable (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.retaildive.com%2Fnews%2Flower-income-households-financially-vulnerable-financial-health-network%2F831129%2F%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0d324cd8b-f1aefd5a-1b56-4072-9ac9-a95416a43e45-000000/iYiX7NX87_IKyXutybxtYm9_ScBHyhBQ9hy3GUa9nXo=452)

Financially vulnerable US households rose from 15% to 17% in 2026, with fewer than 70% reporting they paid all bills on time over the past year. Low-income consumers have shifted more than $10 billion from discretionary purchases to essentials since 2019, driven by a 35% price rise for that group since January 2018. While overall consumer spending remains steady, many are tapping into savings—a trend that may prove unsustainable if costs continue to rise.

### [California will fine creators $5,000 a post for hidden political ads (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fthenextweb.com%2Fnews%2Fcalifornia-ab-1130-paid-political-posts-influencer-fine%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0d324cd8b-f1aefd5a-1b56-4072-9ac9-a95416a43e45-000000/6D4Ycp2KXeqUJ9gO3WJC2clVjhr1i6zXJ_I5KXK0GIQ=452)

California now enforces fines of up to $5,000 per post for creators who fail to disclose paid political content from campaigns. AB 1130 grants enforcement powers to the Fair Political Practices Commission for content paid by campaign committees supporting or opposing candidates or ballot measures, excluding unpaid opinions.

## Strategies & Tactics

### [The Perfectly Optimized Page for AEO (2 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fnectivdigital.com%2Fblog%2Fthe-perfectly-optimized-page-for-aeo-ai-search%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0d324cd8b-f1aefd5a-1b56-4072-9ac9-a95416a43e45-000000/muNZj1Xw42Oig0j7oYgYfhzvSMjKDTnKXtl5lxNav2o=452)

A visual checklist details the six key elements for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO). Server-side rendering and permission for major AI crawlers make pages accessible, while 4–6 direct sentences near the top give AI concise answers to extract. Tables, lists, clear heading hierarchies, and FAQs help parse information, and pages under three months old are reportedly 3x more likely to earn citations.

### [I Built a Growth Team of Agents To Run a 6 Figures Business (6 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ftheindiepreneur.substack.com%2Fp%2Fi-built-growth-team-out-agents-here-exactly-works%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0d324cd8b-f1aefd5a-1b56-4072-9ac9-a95416a43e45-000000/l6O2JWGQtQWH-mlNxAZx5yIv3r2UseocQyr3t0wryOU=452)

An agent growth team can automate drafting and routing, though final sending should remain human-operated. One solo operator pairs an orchestrator with roughly ten channel agents (covering SEO, paid media, and lifecycle email), each tied to a share of monthly growth targets. Tasks are tracked on a board that separates decisions from execution, automatically triggering subsequent agent follow-ups upon completion so the founder only has to review and send.

### [What Would a Child-Free Internet Look Like? (4 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.wired.me%2Fstory%2Fwhat-would-a-child-free-internet-look-like%3Futm_source=tldrmarketing/1/010001a0d324cd8b-f1aefd5a-1b56-4072-9ac9-a95416a43e45-000000/KrY_ilQGIFWKV6acv390mvcqU8BfkfJbYU7KgqV1I6k=452)
