### [Bug blindness (35 minute read)](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fdanluu.com%2Fbug-blind%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a0cdebc7e8-9ae0355f-9134-409f-b680-bb605b2518d7-000000/3u0DmUnwNMlQX1n0n_MTHGYns7wiYw7sPJRayTbpiP4=452)
Some bugs give normal users such a bad experience that they tell their friends about it. Developers and advanced users can be blind to these bugs because they're used to performing the non-intuitive workarounds required to get around them. Humans have a high ability to ignore the negatives in things they like. Being open to criticism can help developers notice more flaws in their work.
