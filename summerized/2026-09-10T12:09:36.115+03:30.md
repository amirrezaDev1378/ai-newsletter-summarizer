## Engineering and Applications

### [Benchmarking LiDAR SLAM on Livox Mid-360 Datasets](https://github.com/harshareddy1611/livox-mid360-slam-evaluation?utm_source=tldrhardware)
This open-source project evaluates four major LiDAR SLAM algorithms—KISS-ICP, DLIO, FAST-LIO2, and GLIM—using aerial drone data. Tested on a Jetson Orin NX platform, the benchmark compares trajectory accuracy and map quality against VICON ground-truth data, providing actionable insights for developers working on compute-constrained autonomous platforms.

## Research and Prototypes

### [RoboTok: An Internet-Scale Data Engine for Physical AI](https://rice-robotpi-lab.github.io/RoboTok/?utm_source=tldrhardware)
Researchers at Rice University developed RoboTok, an automated engine that processes large-scale human demonstration videos from the internet to train robot manipulation policies. By extracting trajectories and interactions from uncurated web video, the system bypasses the expensive bottleneck of physical teleoperation for data collection.

### [Orion: A DIY 12-DOF Quadruped Robot Dog](https://github.com/AshishA26/Orion-Quadruped?utm_source=tldrhardware)
Orion is an open-source, 12-degree-of-freedom quadruped robot built using ROS 2, 3D-printed parts, and serial bus servos. The repository includes CAD models, kinematic calculations, and firmware guides, offering a low-cost reference architecture for legged locomotion research.
